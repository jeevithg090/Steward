---
name: gather-accomplishments
description: Collect calendar events and email mentions of completed work from the last 30 days.
---

1. Run calendar_list to get the last 30 days of events. 2. If the list is empty, tell the person and stop. 3. Run mail_list to see the person's recent emails. 4. For each email, check the subject and body for words like 'finished', 'completed', 'award', or 'milestone'. 5. Collect up to five accomplishments, noting the date and a one-sentence summary. 6. If fewer than three are found, tell the person how many were located.
