# green bond scout

Finds green or ESG-labeled bonds that match your credit quality and maturity criteria.

**What it does:** Searches the web for bonds labeled as green or ESG and checks their credit rating and maturity date against your rules.

**What it needs:** web_search, browser_navigate, browser_snapshot, show_table.

**What it will not do:** Give financial advice, guarantee returns, or buy bonds for you. It cannot check bonds that are not publicly listed.
