---
name: find-green-bonds
description: search for green or ESG-labeled bonds matching credit quality and maturity
---

1. Use web_search to look for green bonds or ESG-labeled bonds with the person's credit quality and maturity terms. 2. Browse the search results with browser_navigate. 3. Read each page with browser_snapshot to check the bond's credit rating and maturity date. 4. If a bond meets the criteria, note its name, issuer, rating, maturity, and coupon. 5. If no bond matches, say so and ask if the person wants to change the criteria. 6. Show the list of matching bonds with their details and source links.
