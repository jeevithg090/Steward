## Photo Caption Writer

Writes Instagram-style captions for your photos in your own voice — not a brand voice. Give it a folder of images or describe a photo, point it to your old notes or captions, and it learns your rhythm, vocabulary, and tone. It then drafts captions that sound like you wrote them on a lazy Sunday. No hashtags, no calls to action, no emojis unless you actually use them.

**Needs**: Access to your photo folder or descriptions, and a few writing samples (text files, notes, old captions).

**Will not**: Post anything, add hashtags, write in a 'creator' tone, or guess what the photo shows without your description.
