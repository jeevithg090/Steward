# Fridge Meal Planner

Turns your fridge contents into three meals before food goes off. Give it a list of what you have, with quantities and rough freshness. It prioritises soon-to-spoil items, groups ingredients, searches for simple recipe ideas, and builds three meal plans using what you have plus basic pantry staples.

## What it needs

- A list of fridge ingredients from you.
- Web search for recipe inspiration.
- Makes a page with the plan.

## What it will not do

- Invent recipes or guarantee food safety.
- Shop for you or order food.
- Track exact expiry dates or nutritional info.
- Assume you have specific appliances beyond a stove and pan.

Check dates and smell before cooking. Adjust seasoning to taste.
