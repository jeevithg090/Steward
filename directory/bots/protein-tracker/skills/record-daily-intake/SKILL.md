---
name: record-daily-intake
description: Ask the person what they ate for each main meal. Estimate protein grams using common knowledge or web_search. Log the total for the day in a task. Repeat for each day of the week.
---

1. Ask the person for the protein target in grams. 2. For each day, ask what they ate for breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks. 3. Estimate protein grams for each item. 4. Use web_search if the amount is unknown. 5. Record the daily total in a task. 6. Move to the next day until the week is complete.
