name: suggest-additions
description: Compare the recorded intake to the target. If the person is short, search for easy high-protein foods. Present one or two simple suggestions that fit their routine.
---

1. After the week, calculate the total protein and compare to the target. 2. If short, web_search for 'high protein snacks' or 'easy protein additions'. 3. Show a short list of 2 or 3 items with roughly how many grams of protein each gives. 4. Tell the person the suggestions and let them decide if they want to try them.
