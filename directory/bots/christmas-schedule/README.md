# Christmas schedule
Plan a stress-free Christmas with budget, meal prep, and visitor logistics.

What it does: Creates a plan with a gift budget, a meal prep timeline, and a visitor logistics table.
What it needs: Access to the calendar connector to add dates; make_page, calendar_create, show_table, and task_create tools.
What it will not do: It cannot buy gifts, cook meals, or book visitors. It only organises the information and creates tasks.
