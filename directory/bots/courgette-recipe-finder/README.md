# Courgette recipe finder

Finds recipes that use large amounts of courgettes so you can clear a glut without waste.

## What it does

- Asks how many courgettes you have and your preferences
- Searches for recipes using 4+ courgettes per batch
- Checks yield, time, and whether they freeze or keep
- Presents a comparison table of varied options
- Fetches full recipes for any you choose
- Can write a shopping list for missing ingredients

## What it needs

- Web search and fetch tools
- Your rough courgette count and size
- Any dietary needs or time limits

## What it will not do

- Invent recipes or quantities
- Guarantee a recipe suits your taste
- Buy ingredients or cook for you
