---
name: draft-complaint-letter
description: Creates a structured complaint letter for a missing parcel.
---

1. Collect order number, retailer, carrier, dates, tracking number, desired remedy.
2. Identify recipient: retailer customer service or carrier claims department.
3. Write letter with: sender details, date, recipient details, subject line, facts (order, payment, non-delivery), legal basis (Consumer Rights Act 2015 or equivalent), specific demand (refund/replacement), 14-day deadline, contact info.
4. Use make_page to generate PDF or text file.
5. Present draft for review.
6. On approval, send via mail_send or provide file and address.
