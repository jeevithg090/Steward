# Parcel complaint writer

Writes and sends a formal complaint letter for a parcel that never arrived. You provide the order details; the bot drafts the letter citing consumer rights, asks for your approval, then emails it to the retailer or carrier.

## What it needs
- Order number, retailer name, purchase date, expected delivery date.
- Tracking number and carrier (if known).
- Whether you want a refund, replacement, or investigation.
- Access to your email to send the letter.

## What it will not do
- Contact the carrier by phone or chat.
- File claims on marketplace platforms (e.g. eBay, Amazon) — it only sends email.
- Guarantee a refund; it only writes and sends the request.
