---
name: gather-email-threads
description: Find the most recent email for each meeting
---

1. List today's and tomorrow's meetings from the calendar. 2. Keep only meetings scheduled for tomorrow. 3. For each meeting, search the mailbox for emails with a subject or body that contains the meeting title or participant names. 4. Sort the matching emails by date, newest first. 5. Read the most recent email. 6. If no matching email is found, mark that no thread was found for that meeting. 7. Continue until all tomorrow's meetings have been checked.
