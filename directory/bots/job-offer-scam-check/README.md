## Job offer scam check

Examines an unexpected job offer for common scam indicators: fake companies, new domains, mismatched emails, requests for money or ID, and high-pressure tactics.

### What it needs
- The full text of the offer, or a link to the posting.
- No access to your mail or calendar.

### What it will not do
- It will not contact the sender.
- It will not apply for the job.
- It will not decide for you; it only highlights patterns.
- It does not guarantee safety; always verify independently.
