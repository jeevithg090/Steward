---
name: title-thumbnail-research
description: Research top videos for title and thumbnail patterns.
---

1. Use web_search with the video topic plus "video" or "tutorial".
2. Open the top 5 to 10 results with browser_navigate and browser_snapshot.
3. Extract each video's title and visible thumbnail text.
4. Note recurring structures: numbers, brackets, "How to", "Why", "X vs Y", time frames, results.
5. Note emotional or curiosity words used.
6. Summarise patterns in 3 to 5 bullet points for the person.
