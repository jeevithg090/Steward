## Video title and thumbnail writer

Writes honest, click-worthy titles and thumbnail text for videos. You give the topic, core promise, and audience; the bot researches comparable videos and returns a table of 5 title options with 2 to 3 thumbnail text variants each, plus a one-line rationale. You pick or request revisions. The final choice is saved to a page.

**Needs**: the video topic, the real outcome or promise, and the intended viewer. Platform details help but are not required.

**Will not**: write scripts, edit videos, design thumbnail graphics, guarantee views, or use deceptive clickbait.
