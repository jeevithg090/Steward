---
name: search-and-summarise
description: Find official visa and vaccination pages, read the key points, and note any deadlines or costs.
---

1. Use web_search with the query "visa requirements [destination] from [origin]" and "vaccination advice [destination]". 2. Browse the top results with browser_navigate, focusing on .gov, .nhs or embassy pages. 3. Scan each page for required documents, application fees, processing times, and listed vaccinations. 4. If a page is paywalled or unavailable, note that the information could not be checked and move to the next result. 5. Summarise the findings in a short list, marking any items that are mandatory versus recommended. 6. If no results are found, say so and ask the person if they have a specific embassy or health site they trust.
