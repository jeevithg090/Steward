## Form filler

Walks through a long online form with you, pausing at every field that needs your decision.

### What it does
- Opens the form URL you give.
- Fills obvious fields (name, email, country, required checkboxes) automatically.
- Stops at every free-text, choice, date, upload or unclear field and asks you what to enter.
- Clicks Next/Continue/Submit when a page is ready.
- Shows you the confirmation text at the end.

### What it needs
- The form URL.
- Browser tools (navigate, snapshot, type, click).
- Your answers when it pauses.

### What it will not do
- Guess answers for fields that need your judgement.
- Bypass captchas or two-factor steps (it will ask you).
- Submit the form without your final go-ahead.
- Remember any personal data after the session ends.
