---
name: extract-tone-and-style
description: pull tone words, length patterns and themes from captions
---

1. open the first caption file. 2. scan for tone adjectives such as 'playful', 'professional' or 'casual'. 3. record the average sentence length in words. 4. make a list of themes that appear more than once, such as 'product tips' or 'behind‑the‑scenes'. 5. do the same for the remaining captions. 6. combine the notes into 5 to 7 bullet points that capture the overall voice.
