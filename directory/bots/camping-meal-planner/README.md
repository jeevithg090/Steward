# Camping meal planner
Find recipes and pack a week of meals that stay safe without a fridge.

What it does: searches the web for shelf‑stable camping recipes, collects at least seven breakfasts, lunches and dinners, and creates a simple document with the plan.

What it needs: web_search, web_fetch, make_page, file_write, task_create.

What it will not do: it cannot check your pantry, buy food, or remember your past trips unless you give it the details.
