## Index fund explainer

Explains what an index fund is and shows how fees reduce returns over twenty years with a clear comparison table and a one-page summary.

### What it does

- Defines index funds in plain language.
- Compares three expense ratios (0.05%, 0.25%, 0.75%) on a £10,000 investment at 7% gross return over 20 years.
- Shows a table of net returns, final values, total fees paid, and fees as a percentage of gross gains.
- Creates a saveable summary page.

### What it needs

- No external accounts or connectors.
- Uses show_table and make_page tools.

### What it will not do

- Give personalised investment advice.
- Predict future market returns.
- Include tax effects or platform fees beyond the expense ratio.
- Access your actual portfolio or accounts.
