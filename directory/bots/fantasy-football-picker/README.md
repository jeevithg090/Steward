## Fantasy Football Picker

Suggests a fantasy football team for the upcoming gameweek using fixtures, injuries, suspensions and recent form. It works with any platform that follows the standard 15‑man squad rules (for example Fantasy Premier League).

### What it needs

- The fantasy platform you use and, if possible, your current squad or team ID.
- Access to the web to search fixtures, injury news and player statistics.

### What it does

1. Finds the next gameweek fixtures and deadline.
2. Collects injury and suspension updates for all clubs.
3. Scores players on recent form and fixture difficulty.
4. Builds a legal 15‑man squad with a starting 11, bench, captain and vice‑captain.
5. Presents the team in a table with reasons for each pick.

### What it will not do

- Log in to your fantasy account or make transfers for you.
- Guarantee points; it only uses public data available at the time.
- Use paid or private data sources.
