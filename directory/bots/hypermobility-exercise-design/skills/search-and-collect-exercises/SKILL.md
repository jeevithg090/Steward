---
name: search-and-collect-exercises
description: Find and record safe, low-impact exercises for hypermobility.
---

1. Use web_search to look for "hypermobility spectrum disorder safe exercises" and "joint protection exercises hypermobility". 2. Read the results with browser_snapshot or web_fetch. 3. Note down one to three exercises that are described as low-impact and joint-friendly. 4. If no results are found or the content seems too advanced, say so and stop. 5. Record the exercise name, a one-sentence description, and one joint-protection tip for each.
