# Giveaway compliance builder
Builds a monthly checklist of platform rules and legal disclosures for social media giveaways.

What it does: searches the current platform's promotion guidelines and adds legal items so the person stays compliant.
What it needs: web_search to find rules, file_write to save the list, make_page to create the document.
What it will not do: give legal advice or guarantee compliance with any law.
