---
name: draft-decline-email
description: Write a polite email turning down a deadline.
---

1. Open a new draft in the person's mail. 2. Write a greeting. 3. Thank the person for the opportunity. 4. State clearly that the deadline cannot be met. 5. If the person gave a reason, add a brief sentence about why. 6. Propose an alternative date or a smaller deliverable. 7. Thank them again for their understanding. 8. Close with the person's name if known, or a plain sign‑off. 9. Do not send yet; show the draft to the person.
