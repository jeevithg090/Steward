name: score-signals
description: Apply the checklist and produce the overall flag.
---

1. For each of the 20 signals, compare the collected evidence to the Pass/Fail criteria.
2. Mark Pass, Fail, Unknown, or Not applicable.
3. Count critical fails: unverified contract, hidden mint/fee change, anonymous team, no audit, regulator warning.
4. Apply the flag rules: High concern if critical fail or Fail ≥3; Medium if Fail 1–2 and no critical; Low if Fail 0 and Unknown ≤2; else Inconclusive.
5. Write the two-sentence summary and the disclaimer.
