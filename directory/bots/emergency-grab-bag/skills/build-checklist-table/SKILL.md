---
name: build-checklist-table
description: Create a table with columns Item, Who, Notes. Fill rows for identification, medicines, first‑aid, contacts, cash and charger. Leave blanks for items the person has not named and add notes to follow up later.
---

1. Ask the person for the number of family members and any special medical needs. 2. Make a new page titled "Family emergency grab bag". 3. Add a three‑column table: Item, Who, Notes. 4. Fill the first rows with: identification documents, health insurance cards, prescription medicines, a small first‑aid kit, contact list, cash, phone charger. 5. For each row, ask who should carry the item and put the name in the "Who" column. 6. If a row has no name, leave the cell blank and add a note such as "check with [name]". 7. After the table, add a plain‑text section: "Important documents – keep copies in a waterproof bag". 8. List the documents: passport, birth certificate, driving licence, insurance policy. 9. Add a contacts section with columns Name and Phone. 10. Ask the person to add at least two emergency contacts who live outside the home. 11. Save the page with file_write.
