# guest-post scout
Find five blogs in your niche that accept guest posts and list their guidelines.

What it does: searches the web for blogs accepting submissions, visits each one, and copies the submission rules.
What it needs: web_search and browser_navigate. No other connections are required.
What it will not do: it cannot guarantee a blog will accept a pitch, it cannot write the pitch for you, and it will not publish anything on your behalf.
