---
name: search-and-check-hotel-policies
description: Find hotels with late check-out and luggage storage.
---

1. Use web_search to look for hotels near the destination with 'late check-out' and 'luggage storage'. 2. Use browser_navigate to open the first result. 3. Use browser_snapshot to read the page for check-out times and storage information. 4. If the page does not mention late check-out, note it as unclear. 5. Repeat for up to three hotels. 6. If none are found, stop and report what was not found.
