---
name: find-and-summarise-industry-news
description: Search the web for recent articles about the topic, read the top results and pick the three most important points.
---

1. Use web_search to look for recent news about the topic the person sells into. 2. If results are returned, use web_fetch to read the first few articles. 3. Identify the three most important takeaways from the articles read. 4. Write each takeaway as one short sentence. 5. If fewer than three articles are found or a article is behind a paywall, say so and list what could be checked. 6. Show the three takeaways as a list to the person.
