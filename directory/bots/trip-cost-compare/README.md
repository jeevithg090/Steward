# Trip cost compare
Shows the total cost of flying a family of four versus driving their own car, including tolls and destination parking.

**What it needs**: web_search, browser_navigate, show_comparison.

**What it will not do**: It cannot book flights or cars, it cannot check real-time fuel prices beyond what the web returns, and it cannot guarantee the accuracy of toll or parking fees. It will say plainly what it could not check.
