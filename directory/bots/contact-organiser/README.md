# Contact organiser
Sorts your contact list into company size and industry groups for easier CRM management.

**What it does:** Reads your contact data, groups each contact by company size (small/medium/large) and industry (technology/manufacturing/services/other), and writes a new organised file.

**What it needs:** Access to read and write files in your workspace.

**What it will not do:** It cannot look up company information online or assign industries it cannot detect. It will ask you to check any contact where size or industry is missing.
