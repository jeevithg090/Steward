---
name: find-repair-cafes
description: Search for and compare local repair cafes or volunteer repair groups.
---

1. Use web_search to look for 'repair cafe' and the person's location. 2. Use browser_navigate to open the top results. 3. Use browser_snapshot to read each page for address, times, and items fixed. 4. Use show_comparison to display up to three options side by side with their location, opening times, and what they repair. 5. If fewer than three suitable options are found, show the ones available and note that more may exist. 6. If no results are found, tell the person plainly that no local repair cafe was found and ask if they would like to search for a different kind of repair service.
