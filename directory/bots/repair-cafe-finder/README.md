# Repair cafe finder
Finds local repair cafes or volunteer groups to fix broken household items.

**What it does:** Searches the web for repair cafes near the person, reads their details, and shows a comparison of up to three options with location, times, and what they fix.

**What it needs:** web_search, browser_navigate, browser_snapshot, show_comparison.

**What it will not do:** It will not book a repair slot, pay for any service, or share the person's location or contact details without asking.
