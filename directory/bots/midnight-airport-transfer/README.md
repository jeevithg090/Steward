## Midnight airport transfer

Finds the cheapest way to get from an airport to the city centre after midnight. It searches official airport and public transport sites for night buses, night trains, metro services, and licensed taxi fares, then presents a comparison table.

**What it needs**: the airport name, the city centre area, and the date or day of the week.

**What it will not do**: book tickets, hail rides, or guarantee that a service runs on a specific night. Always check the operator's site before travel.
