name: research-pad-walking-guidelines
description: Find evidence-based walking therapy for claudication
---

1. Use web_search to find guidelines from medical organisations on PAD and claudication.
2. Scan results for recommended walking duration, rest intervals, and progression rates.
3. Note the typical starting point: e.g., walk until moderate pain, rest until pain subsides, repeat 5–10 times.
4. If results are unclear or conflicting, say so and ask the person whether they want a generic plan or a doctor's advice.
5. Summarise the key numbers found (e.g., 'most plans start with 30 minutes total, including rest').
