name: script-structure
description: Breaks a five-minute runtime into timed script sections with word counts.
---

1. Hook: 75 words (0:00–0:30).
2. Context: 150 words (0:30–1:30).
3. Core section 1: 150 words (1:30–2:30).
4. Core section 2: 150 words (2:30–3:30).
5. Core section 3: 125 words (3:30–4:15) or split into two 75-word sections.
6. Recap: 50 words (4:15–4:30).
7. Call to action: 50 words (4:30–5:00).
   Total ~750 words. Adjust per person's pacing notes.
