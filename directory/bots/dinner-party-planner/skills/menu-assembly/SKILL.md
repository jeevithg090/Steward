---
name: menu-assembly
description: Combines chosen recipes into a menu with shopping list and timeline.
---

1. Take the chosen recipes for each course.
2. Merge ingredients into a combined shopping list, grouping by category (produce, pantry, etc.).
3. Scale quantities to the total guest count.
4. Create a prep timeline: list tasks in order, working backwards from the desired serve time, noting what can be done ahead.
5. Add cross-contamination notes: separate utensils, prep areas, and cooking order.
6. Use make_page to produce the final document.
