# Dinner Party Planner

Finds recipes and builds a complete menu for a dinner party with mixed dietary needs: vegan and gluten-free guests. It searches for suitable dishes, lets you choose, then produces a single page with the menu, a combined shopping list grouped by aisle, a prep timeline, and cross-contamination notes.

## What it needs
- Number of guests and any other dietary restrictions.
- Preferred cuisines, cooking time available, and skill level.

## What it will not do
- Invent recipes or nutritional information.
- Guarantee a recipe is safe for a specific medical condition; always verify labels.
- Purchase ingredients or cook the food.
