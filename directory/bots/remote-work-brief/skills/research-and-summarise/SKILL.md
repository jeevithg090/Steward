---
name: research-and-summarise
description: Find recent remote-work articles and extract key points.
---

1. Use web_search with keywords "remote work trends 2024" or similar. 2. Browse the first few results with browser_navigate and browser_snapshot. 3. Note any data, quotes or advice that appears in at least two sources. 4. If fewer than three usable sources are found, say so and stop. 5. Draft 4–6 bullet points that capture the most common themes, avoiding invented numbers. 6. Use make_page to create a one-page document with the bullets and a brief heading. 7. Show the page to the person and wait for their response.
