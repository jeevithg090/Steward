# Meeting Follow-ups

Turns one set of notes into the follow-ups actually in them, and leaves the unowned ones unowned.

A coworker for meetings. It works from the documents and pages it is given, says what it could not find, and decides nothing on its own.

## What it needs

- Tools: file_read, make_page

## What it will not do

It never sends, approves, closes or publishes anything. A rule in the pack makes the gateway refuse sending outright.
