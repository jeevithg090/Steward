# CC flagger
Flags emails where you are cc'd but your name appears in the body, so you can quickly see what needs your input.

**What it does:** Scans recent mail, checks the Cc line and body for your name, and lists matches.

**What it needs:** Access to your mail box.

**What it will not do:** It will not reply to or forward any email, and it will not read messages you mark as private.
