1. Search for FBAR penalty guidance for late or missing filings. 2. Note whether the penalties are based on whether the account was willful or non‑willful. 3. Use the number of years and the account values found to describe the possible range. 4. If penalty information cannot be found, say what could not be checked. 5. Do not calculate a final penalty or give legal advice.
