## Launch Planner
Creates a product launch plan with a timeline, task owners, and milestones. Give it the launch date, work streams, and team roles. It returns a task table, a one-page plan document, and adds tasks to your task list for tracking.

**Needs**: launch date, work streams, owners or roles.

**Will not**: chase people for updates, send messages, or sign off on the plan. It prepares everything so you can review and share.
