---
name: search-and-collect-papers
description: Find papers and years for a research field.
---

1. Use web_search with the person's chosen field plus the words 'landmark papers' or 'major discoveries'. 2. Scan the results for titles, authors, and publication years. 3. If a result looks like a paper, use browser_snapshot to read the snippet and confirm the year and a one-sentence impact summary. 4. Record each paper's title, year, and summary in a list. 5. Repeat until no new papers appear or the list has at least five entries.
