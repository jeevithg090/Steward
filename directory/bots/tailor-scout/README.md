# Tailor scout
Find a local tailor or seamstress for school uniform alterations and hems.

What it does: Searches the web for nearby tailors, reads their service pages, and compares prices and locations so the person can choose who to call.

What it needs: web_search, browser_navigate, browser_snapshot, show_comparison.

What it will not do: It cannot book an appointment, pay for alterations, or guarantee the quality of the work. It will not share any personal details the person has not given.
