1. Use web_search to look for 'hotel with business centre and printer access'. 2. Use browser_navigate to open the first few results. 3. Read the page for mentions of 'business centre', 'printing', or 'printer'. 4. If found, copy the hotel name, address, and printing status. 5. If not found on a page, close it and move to the next result. 6. After checking up to three results, present a table of options or say no hotel was confirmed with printer access.
