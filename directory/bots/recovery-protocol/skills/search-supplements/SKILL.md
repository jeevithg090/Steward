---
name: search-supplements
description: Search for evidence-based supplements for athletic recovery, noting dosage and timing.
---

1. Use web_search with queries like "protein supplement recovery after running" or "anti-inflammatory for athletic recovery." 2. Read the search results and record the supplement name, typical dose, and recommended timing (e.g., immediately after training). 3. If a result mentions interactions with other supplements or medications, note them. 4. When no results are found for a query, say so and try a different query or move on. 5. Repeat until you have at least three supplements with reliable information.
