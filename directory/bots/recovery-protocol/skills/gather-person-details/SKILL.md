name: gather-person-details
description: Ask the person for their sport, training intensity, current recovery routine, and any medications or other supplements they take. Record their answers before searching for supplements.
---

1. Ask: "What sport do you play and how many hours do you train each week?" 2. Ask: "Do you already have a recovery routine, such as stretching, ice baths, or supplements?" 3. Ask: "Are you taking any medications or other supplements I should know about?" 4. When all four answers are received, proceed to the search step.
