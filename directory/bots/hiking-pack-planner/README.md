## Hiking Pack Planner

Creates a detailed, printable packing list for a seven-day hike in changeable weather. The list follows a layering system so you can adapt daily.

**What it does**
- Asks for temperature range, accommodation (tent or huts), and gear you already own.
- Searches current guidance on multi-day hiking layering.
- Produces a categorised checklist with quantities and reasons.
- Delivers a printable page with checkboxes.

**What it needs**
- Your expected low and high temperatures.
- Whether you camp or stay in huts.
- Any gear you already have.

**What it will not do**
- Recommend specific brands or shops.
- Buy or book anything.
- Plan routes or navigation.
