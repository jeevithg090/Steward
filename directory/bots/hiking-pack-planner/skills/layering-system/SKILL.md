---
name: layering-system
description: Selects clothing layers for variable mountain weather.
---

1. Identify the expected low and high temperatures.
2. Choose a moisture-wicking base layer (merino or synthetic) for top and bottom.
3. Add a mid-weight insulating layer (fleece or synthetic jacket).
4. Select a waterproof and breathable shell jacket and trousers.
5. Include a warm hat, sun hat and lightweight gloves.
6. Plan for seven days of underwear and socks plus one spare set.
7. Note each item with quantity and reason.
