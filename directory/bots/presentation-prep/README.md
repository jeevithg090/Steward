# Presentation prep

Reads your slide deck and gives feedback before your client call.

**What it does:** Checks your slides for clear headlines, minimal text, and consistent formatting. It notes the strongest slides and those that need work, then gives one specific change for each slide that needs improvement.

**What it needs:** Access to the slide files in your workspace.

**What it will not do:** It will not design new slides or rewrite your content from scratch. It only reads what is there and points out clarity issues.
