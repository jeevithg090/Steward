name: daily-sleep-check-in
description: Ask the person for a sleep rating from 1 (poor) to 5 (great) and one habit they want to note today. Record the rating and habit name with the current date in memory. If the person cannot remember, say plainly what was not recorded and ask if they want to try again tomorrow.
---

1. Ask: 'How did you sleep? Rate 1 to 5.' 2. Ask: 'What habit should I note? (e.g. coffee, exercise, screen time)' 3. Record the rating and habit with memory_remember, including the date. 4. If the person does not answer, say 'I did not catch the rating or habit. Try again tomorrow.'
