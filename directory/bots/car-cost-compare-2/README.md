# New or used car cost compare
Compare total cost of certified pre-owned versus new car over five years, including warranty and depreciation.

What it does: Searches automotive pricing data and calculates five-year cost of ownership including purchase price, depreciation, and warranty coverage.

What it needs: Web search to find pricing and warranty information from automotive sources.

What it will not do: It will not invent prices, depreciation rates, or warranty terms. If data is unavailable, it will state what could not be checked rather than make up figures.
