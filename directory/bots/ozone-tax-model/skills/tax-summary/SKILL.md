description: Summarise the tax treatment of qualified opportunity zone investments
---

1. Search the web for "qualified opportunity zone tax treatment" and open the top results. 2. Snapshot the pages and extract the key rules on deferral, step-up in basis and gain exclusion. 3. If the page does not contain the rules, say you could not find the official guidance and ask if the person has a source. 4. Write a short plain‑language summary of how the tax deferral works while the investment is held, how the basis steps up each year, and what gain is excluded if the investment is held for at least ten years. 5. If any figure (such as the exact basis percentage or the holding period required for exclusion) is not clear, say so and ask the person to supply the missing detail.
