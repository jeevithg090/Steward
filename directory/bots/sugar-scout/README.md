# Sugar scout
Find hidden added sugars in your pantry and suggest lower‑sugar swaps.

What it does: searches the web for the sugar content of your pantry items, compares them to common alternatives, and shows a table with the savings.
What it needs: web_search to look up sugar data, file_read to read your list, make_page to display the results, show_table to present the comparison.
What it will not do: it will not access your shopping accounts, place orders, or give medical advice.
