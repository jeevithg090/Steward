1. Look at the items that have a sugar amount. 2. For each, think of a typical swap (e.g., sugary cereal → plain oats, soda → sparkling water, sweetened yogurt → plain yogurt with fruit). 3. web_search the alternative to confirm its sugar amount. 4. Calculate the sugar saved: original sugar minus alternative sugar. 5. If you cannot find a lower‑sugar version, write "no lower‑sugar swap found". 6. Keep the list short: aim for 3 to 5 swaps.
