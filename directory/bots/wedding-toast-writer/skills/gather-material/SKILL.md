description: Collect stories, voice traits, and constraints for the toast.
---

1. Ask for the desired tone: funny, heartfelt, short, long, or a mix.
2. Request 3 to 5 specific memories with sensory details.
3. Ask how the person speaks: catchphrases, humour, sentence length, language level.
4. Ask about the sister's partner and one telling moment.
5. List topics to avoid.
6. Confirm target length in minutes or words.
7. Store key voice notes with memory_remember for reuse.
