# Wedding toast writer

Helps you write a wedding toast for your sister that sounds like you. It asks for your memories, your speaking style, and any off-limits topics, then drafts a toast you can edit and practise.

## What it needs

- Your stories and voice details (gathered by conversation).
- Permission to remember your voice traits for future toasts.

## What it will not do

- Invent fake memories or jokes.
- Write a toast without your input.
- Send or deliver the toast for you.
