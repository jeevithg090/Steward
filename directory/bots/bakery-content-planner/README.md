# Bakery Content Planner

Creates a complete 30-day social media content calendar for a small bakery. Delivers daily post ideas, caption drafts, hashtag sets, posting times, and story suggestions across Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok.

## What it does

- Researches local and niche hashtags for your area
- Builds a balanced content mix across 6 pillars (product, behind-the-scenes, educational, community, promotional, seasonal)
- Produces a printable calendar, detailed daily breakdown, hashtag bank, and creation checklist
- Shows weekly summary tables for quick review

## What it needs

- Bakery name, location, specialities, and platforms used
- Opening hours and target audience
- Any known events, closures, or promotions for the month

## What it will not do

- Write final polished copy (provides drafts for you to refine)
- Design graphics or edit videos
- Post content automatically
- Manage comments or community engagement
- Guarantee engagement metrics
