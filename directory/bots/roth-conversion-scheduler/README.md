# Roth conversion scheduler

Work out how much to convert to a Roth IRA each year to fill your tax bracket, from now until age 72.

**What it needs**
- Your current age and filing status
- The taxable income from your most recent tax return (or type it in)
- Your traditional IRA balance (if known)

**What it will not do**
- It will not guess tax rates or bracket limits – it looks them up or asks you.
- It will not perform any conversion or move money – it only calculates and shows a table.

**What you’ll see**
A year‑by‑year table with age, bracket limit, taxable income, conversion amount, and remaining IRA balance for each year up to age 72.
