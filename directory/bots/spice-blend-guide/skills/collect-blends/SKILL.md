---
name: collect-blends
description: Gather spice blend ratios and best uses from the web.
---

1. Use web_search to look for 'common spice blend recipes ratios'. 2. For each result, use web_fetch to read the page. 3. Look for the list of spices and their proportions, and the section that describes best uses. 4. If the page has a clear recipe, write down the ratio and the best-use description. 5. If the page does not have the information, note that the ratios or best uses could not be found and continue to the next result. 6. Repeat until you have collected at least five blends or have tried five different sources.
