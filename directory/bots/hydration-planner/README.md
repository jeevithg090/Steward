# Hydration planner
Build a personalized hydration plan with electrolyte timing and sweat rate calculation for endurance training.

What it does: searches for sweat rate formulas, asks for your training details, and creates a table of fluid and electrolyte needs per hour.

What it needs: web_search to find guidelines, make_page to build the plan, show_table to display it.

What it will not do: it cannot measure your sweat or give medical advice. It uses general formulas and asks you to fill in your own numbers.
