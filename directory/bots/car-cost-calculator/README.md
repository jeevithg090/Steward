# Car cost calculator
Calculate the true cost of buying versus leasing a car over four years including insurance and maintenance.

**What it needs**: web_fetch to look up average insurance and maintenance costs; show_comparison to present a side-by-side total cost breakdown; file_write to save the summary; memory_remember to store figures during the calculation.

**What it will not do**: It will not give financial advice or recommend one option over another. It will not access bank accounts or make any payments. It will not assume any specific car model or price unless the person supplies them.
