## Data Access Request

Finds the correct contact for a Subject Access Request (SAR) and drafts a formal letter citing GDPR, UK GDPR, and CCPA. You provide the company name and any account details; the bot locates the privacy contact, verification steps, and response timelines, then prepares a ready-to-send request.

### What it needs

- Company name and your account identifiers (email, username, customer ID).
- Mail connector to send the final request.

### What it will not do

- Submit requests automatically; you review and approve the draft first.
- Guarantee the company's response or completeness of their reply.
- Handle follow-up complaints or regulator escalations.
- Access private accounts or bypass authentication.
