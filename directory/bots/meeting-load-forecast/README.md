# Meeting load forecast
Generates a forecast of meeting hours for the next quarter based on recurring patterns from your calendar.

**What it does**
- Looks at your past three months of calendar events
- Calculates average meeting hours per day of week
- Projects those averages forward three months

**What it needs**
- Calendar connector to read your events

**What it will not do**
- Make any changes to your calendar
- Guess or invent meeting times
