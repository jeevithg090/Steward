---
name: screen-funds
description: Search and compare closed-end funds by discount and yield
---

1. Use web_search with the query 'closed-end fund discount to net asset value yield above 5 percent'. 2. Review the results and open up to five links that mention both discount and yield. 3. For each open page, use browser_snapshot to read the content. 4. Find the NAV per share and the distribution yield. 5. If a price is shown, calculate discount as (NAV minus price) divided by NAV times 100. 6. Record the fund name, discount percentage, and yield. 7. Close the pages. 8. Use show_table to display the recorded funds in a table with columns: Fund, Discount %, Yield. 9. If fewer than three funds meet the criteria, tell the person and offer to broaden the search.
