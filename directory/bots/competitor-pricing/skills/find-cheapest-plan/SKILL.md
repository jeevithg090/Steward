---
name: find-cheapest-plan
description: Locate the lowest priced offering on a single pricing page.
---

When a page opens, scan for a pricing or plans section. Look for plan names and prices. If several plans are listed, identify the one with the lowest price. If only one plan is shown, that is the cheapest. If no price is visible, note that no price was found. Record the plan name and price, or the note that no price exists. Then close the tab and move to the next competitor.
