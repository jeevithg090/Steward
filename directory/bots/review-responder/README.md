# Review responder
Helps you write honest replies to negative reviews that keep your dignity.

What it does: Takes the review text and your business type, then drafts a short, respectful response. It thanks the reviewer, addresses the concern factually, and invites further talk offline.

What it needs: The review text and the kind of business you run.

What it will not do: It will not make up facts, invent details about what happened, or promise any outcome. It always shows the draft first so you can approve or change it.
