# Trip itinerary builder

Collects travel bookings from your inbox and builds a day-by-day itinerary page. It searches for confirmation emails, extracts dates, times, locations, and references, then organises everything chronologically.

## What it needs
- Access to your mail (to read booking confirmations)
- Access to your calendar (to add events if you want)

## What it will not do
- Guess missing dates or times
- Book or change any travel
- Share your itinerary with anyone
- Access folders you don't point it to
