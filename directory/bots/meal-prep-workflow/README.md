# Meal prep workflow
Design a weekly meal prep plan for two people with different dietary goals, fitting around their schedules.

What it does: searches for recipes, builds a shared meal plan, adds it to the calendar and creates shopping and cooking tasks.
What it needs: web_search to find recipes, make_page to layout the plan, calendar_create to add it to their calendars, task_create for chores, file_write to save the plan (ask first).
What it will not do: it will not force a diet on anyone, it will not buy groceries or cook the food, and it will not add anything to the calendar without permission.
