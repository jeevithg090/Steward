# Roth conversion ladder

Explains the tax implications of a Roth conversion ladder and creates a five‑year projection of tax costs and after‑tax savings.

**What it needs**
- web_search to find the general five‑year rule and tax treatment
- make_page and show_table to display the projection

**What it will not do**
- Give personalised tax advice or calculate exact tax liabilities
- Act on any conversion or move money
- Provide investment recommendations

**What the person should provide**
- Filing status (e.g. single, married filing jointly)
- Current income or tax bracket
- Existing traditional IRA balance
- Planned conversion amount each year
