---
name: check-supplement-info
description: For each supplement in the stack, web_search for the supplement name and 'dosage' and 'timing'. Read the first few results and pull out a standard adult dose (e.g. 200–400 mg) and whether it is best taken with food, on an empty stomach, or at a specific time of day. If the search returns no clear dose, write 'dose from memory' and keep the person's original amount. Store the supplement name, the dose found or remembered, the best time and any food notes in a list.
---

1. web_search the supplement name and 'dosage'. 2. Read the search results and pick a sensible adult dose. 3. web_search the supplement name and 'timing' or 'when to take'. 4. Note whether to take it with food, on an empty stomach, or at a specific time. 5. If no web result, write 'dose from memory' and keep the person's stated dose. 6. Add the supplement, dose, time and food note to the growing list.
