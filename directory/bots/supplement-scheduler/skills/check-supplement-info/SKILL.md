---
name: check-supplement-info
description: For each supplement in the stack, web_search for the supplement name and 'dosage' and 'timing'. Read the first few results and note the usual adult dose range they give (e.g. 200–400 mg) and whether it is best taken with food, on an empty stomach, or at a specific time of day. If the search returns no clear range, say so and keep the person's own amount. Store the supplement name, the person's dose, the range found, the best time and any food notes in a list.
---

1. web_search the supplement name and 'dosage'. 2. Read the search results and note the usual adult dose range they give. Never pick a dose for the person; say their doctor or pharmacist should confirm it. 3. web_search the supplement name and 'timing' or 'when to take'. 4. Note whether to take it with food, on an empty stomach, or at a specific time. 5. If no web result, say so and keep the person's stated dose. 6. Add the supplement, the person's dose, the range found, the time and the food note to the growing list.
