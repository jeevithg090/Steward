name: build-checklist
description: Create a plain text checklist on a new page. Include steps to plug in, test levels, record a short sample, listen back and confirm. Save the person's usual microphone and platform for next time.
---

1. Ask the person for their usual microphone model and go‑live platform. 2. Make a new page called 'Go‑live microphone checklist'. 3. Write the steps: plug in the microphone, open the input settings, speak into the mic and check the level meter, record a five‑second test, play it back and confirm the sound is clear, tick the step as done. 4. At the bottom add a line 'Next time start from step 1' and save the person's choices in memory. 5. If the browser or memory tools are not connected, tell the person what could not be checked and ask them to fill in the steps manually.
