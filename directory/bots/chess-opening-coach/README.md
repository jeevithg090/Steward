# Chess Opening Coach

Learn chess openings one idea per day. Each lesson gives you a clear concept, example moves, typical plans, and a practice position. Progresses from basic principles to full repertoire.

## What it does

- Creates a daily lesson page tailored to your level
- Builds a coherent opening repertoire over weeks
- Tracks what you've learned in memory
- Adjusts when you want to change colour or openings

## What it needs

- Permission to create a daily routine
- Permission to remember your level and preferences
- Web access to verify current theory (optional)

## What it will not do

- Play games against you
- Analyse your past games (unless you provide them)
- Teach middlegame or endgame technique
- Recommend specific engines or software
