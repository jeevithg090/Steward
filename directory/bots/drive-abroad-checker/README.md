## Drive abroad checker

Finds the documents you need to drive a hired car in another country. It checks official government sources for both the destination and your licence-issuing country, covering driving licences, International Driving Permits, insurance, hire car paperwork, and mandatory vehicle equipment.

**What it needs**
- The country you will drive in.
- The country that issued your driving licence.

**What it will not do**
- It cannot guarantee the hire company's own rules; always check your rental agreement.
- It does not book or arrange any permits.
- It does not provide legal advice.
