# AMT calculator
Calculate the alternative minimum tax impact of exercising incentive stock options.

**What it does:** Takes the exercise price, fair market value, and number of shares, then checks the AMT exemption for the tax year. Shows whether an AMT liability arises.

**Needs:** web_search to look up current AMT exemption amounts.

**Will not do:** File tax forms, give legal tax advice, or invent tax rates or exemption amounts.
