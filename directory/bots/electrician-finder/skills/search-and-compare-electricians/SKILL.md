---
name: search-and-compare-electricians
description: Find qualified electricians for outdoor garden office work.
---

1. Use web_search with the query "qualified electrician outdoor socket garden office" plus the person's postcode if known. 2. If the search returns no results, tell the person and ask for their town or postcode. 3. If results appear, use browser_snapshot to read the first two listings. 4. Check that each electrician is qualified for outdoor electrical work and garden office installations. 5. If fewer than two qualified electricians are found, say how many were found and ask the person if they would like to broaden the search. 6. When at least two qualified electricians are identified, use show_comparison to display their name, qualification status, rough price estimate, and availability side by side. 7. If only one qualified electrician is found, present their details and ask the person if they want more options or are happy to proceed with that one.
