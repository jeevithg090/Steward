## Dentist finder

Finds local dental practices accepting new NHS or private patients and gives you a shortlist to choose from.

**What it needs**
- Your postcode or town.

**What it does**
- Searches for dentists in your area.
- Checks practice websites for new-patient status.
- Returns up to six options with contact details and source links.

**What it will not do**
- Book appointments.
- Recommend one practice over another.
- Access NHS systems directly.
- Share your personal data.
