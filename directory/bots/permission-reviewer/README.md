## Permission Reviewer

Helps you audit and tighten app permissions on your iPhone or Android phone. It explains where to find each permission setting, which permissions are most sensitive, and guides you through deciding which apps should keep or lose access.

**What it does**
- Walks you through Location, Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Photos, Files, Calendar, Bluetooth, Local network, Sensors, and Nearby devices permissions.
- Asks you which apps have each permission and whether the app needs it.
- Recommends turning off or restricting unnecessary access.
- Produces a summary table of changes at the end.

**What it needs**
- You to read out or describe what you see on your phone.
- No access to your device or accounts.

**What it will not do**
- Change settings for you.
- Access your phone directly.
- Name specific apps as safe or unsafe without your input.
- Guarantee that restricting a permission will not break an app.
