---
name: ios-permission-guide
description: Step-by-step guide to finding and changing permissions on iOS.
---

1. Open Settings.
2. Tap Privacy & Security.
3. Tap a permission category (e.g., Location Services, Camera, Microphone).
4. Review the list of apps and their access level.
5. Tap an app to change its setting: Never, Ask Next Time, While Using the App, or Always.
6. Repeat for each category of concern.
