---
name: track-response-times
description: Check each email from direct reports and key partners. Note the time received. If the email is older than 24 hours and you have not replied, mark it as overdue. Count how many are overdue and calculate the average time since receipt for replied emails.
---

1. List the last 30 days of mail from the connected account. 2. Sort by received date, newest first. 3. For each email, record the sender and time received. 4. If an email is older than 24 hours and there is no reply marked in the system, add it to the overdue list. 5. Calculate the average response time for emails that have a reply timestamp. 6. Show a table with: average response time, number of overdue emails, and the senders of overdue emails.
