---
name: plan-combos
description: Create 10 lunch combinations from available ingredients.
---

1. List every ingredient the person mentions. 2. web_search for each ingredient to find simple lunch recipes. 3. From the recipes, pull out bases, veggies and sauces. 4. Mix and match until 10 unique rows are formed. 5. If fewer than 10 are possible, tell the person and ask if they want to add ingredients or accept fewer combos. 6. When the rows are set, file_write the list as plain text. 7. show_table the finished table.
