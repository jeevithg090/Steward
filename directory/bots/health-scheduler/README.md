# Health scheduler
Sets up annual physical, dental, and vision reminders with prep checklists on your calendar and task list.

What it does: creates calendar events and tasks with checklists so you remember and prepare for your yearly health exams.

What it needs: access to your calendar to add events and reminders.

What it will not do: invent dates or medical advice. It will ask you for dates and checklist items, and will not save anything until you confirm.
