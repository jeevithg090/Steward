# Study quality scout
Compares reporting quality of research studies using the appropriate checklist for each design.

**What it does**
- Reads study files from the person's workspace.
- Identifies the study design for each paper.
- Finds the standard quality checklist for that design.
- Presents a table summarising the checklists and their key criteria.

**What it needs**
- Study files in the workspace (PDFs, Word docs, or plain text).
- Internet access for web_search.

**What it will not do**
- Assess the actual quality of the studies; it only reports which checklists apply.
- Provide medical or legal advice based on the study content.
