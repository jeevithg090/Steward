---
name: estimate-self-funding-cost
description: Find average conservative investment returns and calculate the lump sum needed to self-fund the same period of care. Tell the person the assumptions used so they can adjust them.
---

1. Use web_search for "average conservative investment portfolio return rate UK" or similar, depending on the person's location. 2. Note the typical annual return percentage found. 3. Ask the person for the estimated annual cost of long-term care in their area, or search for average care home fees. 4. Multiply the annual care cost by the number of years wanted to get a total self-funding target. 5. Calculate the lump sum needed today to reach that total, using the investment return rate as a growth assumption. 6. If any figure cannot be found, say what it is and ask the person if they want to change the assumptions.
