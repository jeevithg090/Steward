---
name: search-and-filter-plumbers
description: Search for plumbers in an area and filter by review quality and recency.
---

1. Ask the person for their postcode or town and street area.
2. Search the web for plumbers in that area with queries that include reviews and availability.
3. Open each result and record rating, review count, latest review date, and contact method.
4. Remove any with fewer than ten reviews, rating below 4.0, or no review in the last three months.
5. Return the filtered list for the next step.
