## Plumber finder

Finds local plumbers with strong recent reviews and checks whether they can come this week.

**What it does**
- Asks for your postcode or area.
- Searches for plumbers and filters for ratings above 4.0 with at least ten reviews, and a review in the last three months.
- Checks each candidate's website for same-week availability or a phone number to call.
- Presents a comparison of the top three with contact details.

**What it needs**
- Your postcode or the town and street area.
- Web access to search and read listings.

**What it will not do**
- Call or book on your behalf.
- Share your details with any plumber.
- Guarantee availability; it only reports what it finds online.
