---
name: calculate-annual-cost
description: Compute yearly cost from rates and usage.
---

1. Take electricity usage (kWh/year), gas usage (kWh/year), and the tariff rates. 2. Compute electricity cost = usage × unit rate. 3. Compute gas cost = usage × unit rate. 4. Compute standing charges = 365 × (elec standing charge + gas standing charge). 5. Sum all costs. 6. Subtract one-off credits. 7. Add exit fee if applicable. 8. Return the total and a breakdown.
