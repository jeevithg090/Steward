## Energy tariff comparator

Compares electricity and gas tariffs for your address and usage, then shows whether switching saves money.

### What it does
- Asks for your postcode, current supplier, tariff, annual usage (or bill amounts), and meter type.
- Searches independent comparison sites and supplier pages.
- Extracts unit rates, standing charges, exit fees, contract lengths and green credentials.
- Calculates estimated annual cost for each deal, including any exit fees.
- Presents a side-by-side table and a plain-language verdict.

### What it needs
- Your postcode and current tariff details.
- Annual kWh usage or recent bill amounts and dates.
- Meter type (standard, economy 7, smart, prepayment).

### What it will not do
- Click "switch" buttons or start a switch.
- Guarantee prices; estimates are based on published rates and your usage.
- Access your supplier account without your help.
- Recommend a specific supplier; it only shows the numbers.
