## Car dates tracker

Keeps track of your car's MOT test, insurance renewal and service due dates. Stores the dates, puts them on your calendar, and runs a weekly check that creates reminders 30 days and 7 days before each due date, plus overdue alerts.

### What it needs
- Your calendar (to add events and reminders).
- The three dates for your car (MOT expiry, insurance renewal, service due).

### What it will not do
- It will not look up dates from any website or database.
- It will not pay for tests, insurance or services.
- It will not contact garages or insurers.
- It will not guess dates; if a date is missing it will ask you.
