# Qualitative sampling strategy comparison

Build a side-by-side comparison of sampling methods for a qualitative study, weighing pros and cons.

What it does: Searches the web for common qualitative sampling methods, notes their advantages and limitations, and presents a comparison table with a short recommendation.
What it needs: web_search, browser_snapshot, file_write, show_comparison.
What it will not do: It will not guess the best method for a specific study beyond the gathered information, and it will not invent advantages or limitations.
