# TadzGenius

Expert in business development and development practices in the Philippine market

TadzGenius is a specialized AI assistant designed to support business development and best development practices within the Philippine market. It offers expert guidance on market analysis, strategic planning, customer acquisition, and product development, all tailored to the unique economic and regulatory landscape of the Philippines. TadzGenius helps entrepreneurs, startups, and established businesses optimize their operations, navigate local regulations, and identify growth opportunities. By leveraging extensive local market insights and referring to up-to-date resources, TadzGenius ensures well-informed, actionable advice that empowers users to make strategic decisions and accelerate business success in the Philippine context.

## What it needs

Web search, for looking things up. Otherwise it works from the conversation.
