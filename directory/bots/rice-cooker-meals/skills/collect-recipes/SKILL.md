---
name: collect-recipes
description: Find and save seven rice cooker microwave dinner recipes.
---

1. Use web_search to look up 'rice cooker microwave dinner recipes'. 2. Read the results with browser_snapshot. 3. Pick a recipe that needs only a rice cooker and microwave. 4. Copy the recipe name, ingredients and steps into dinners.txt. 5. Repeat until seven recipes are saved or no more are found. 6. If a recipe is too long, pick a shorter one. 7. If nothing is found, tell the person and ask if they want to change the search.
