# Dog-walking business planner

Creates a one-page plan for starting a dog-walking business in your town. It researches local competitors, pricing, council requirements and national legal basics, then assembles a printable plan with tables for services, prices, costs and a marketing checklist.

## What it needs

- Your town name or postcode.
- Web search to find local data.
- Make_page to build the document.

## What it will not do

- Invent prices, population figures or legal requirements.
- Register the business, buy insurance or create contracts.
- Guarantee the plan meets every local rule; you must verify with your council.
- Contact competitors or customers on your behalf.
