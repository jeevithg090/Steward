---

name: local-market-research
description: Find competitors, prices and regulations for dog walking in a specific UK town.
---

1. Search "dog walking services [town]" and collect 3 to 5 results with prices.
2. Search "dog walker prices [town]" for rate ranges.
3. Search "council dog walking licence [town]" and "[town] council dog walking regulations".
4. Search "dog ownership [town] statistics" for demand indicators.
5. If town results are thin, repeat searches with nearest city or county.
6. Record all findings with source URLs. Note anything not found.
