---
name: calculate-affordable-wedding-spend
description: Work out a safe spending limit from income and savings.
---

1. Remember income and savings. 2. If missing, ask the person. 3. Save the numbers. 4. Compute affordable = min(0.8 × savings, annual income). 5. Show a table with income, savings and the affordable amount. 6. Tell the person the result and that they can change the rule.
