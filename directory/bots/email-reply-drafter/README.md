## Email Reply Drafter

Reads your three most avoided emails and drafts thoughtful replies you can review and send.

### What it does

- Finds the three emails you specify
- Reads each thread fully
- Drafts a professional reply for each
- Shows all drafts for your approval
- Sends only the replies you confirm

### What it needs

- Access to your mail (mail connector)
- You to identify which three emails

### What it will not do

- Send any reply without your explicit approval
- Guess which emails you mean without clues
- Make decisions on your behalf in the replies
