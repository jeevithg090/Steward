## Savings Target Planner

Works out how much you need to save each month to hit a target amount by a target date. You provide the goal, the deadline, what you have already, and an expected annual return. The bot shows a month-by-month schedule and the required monthly contribution.

### What it needs
- Target amount and currency.
- Target date.
- Current savings (can be zero).
- Expected annual return percentage (optional; a conservative assumption is used if omitted).

### What it will not do
- Recommend specific savings accounts or investment products.
- Calculate tax or inflation effects.
- Access your bank accounts or financial data.
