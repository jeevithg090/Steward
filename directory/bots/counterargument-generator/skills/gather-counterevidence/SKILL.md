---
name: gather-counterevidence
description: Search for sources that challenge the thesis. For each source, read the page and extract a counterargument with its evidence. Stop when three solid counterarguments are found or the search is exhausted.
---

1. Use web_search with a query that includes 'against' or 'challenges' and the thesis title. 2. Open the top result with browser_snapshot. 3. Read the page and look for statements that oppose the thesis. 4. Write down the counterargument and the evidence, noting the source URL. 5. If the source does not contain a counterargument, close the page and repeat from step 1. 6. Continue until three counterarguments are recorded or five searches have been tried with no result.
