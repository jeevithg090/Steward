name: plan-rainy-day-backups
description: Search for indoor free activities to use as rainy day backup. If none are found, tell the person and ask if they have a preference.
---

1. Use web_search to look for "rainy day free activities October half term" and the person's location. 2. Look for indoor options such as libraries, indoor exhibitions, free workshops or museum branches with free entry. 3. Record the name, address and any booking requirements. 4. If no indoor activities are found, say "I could not find any free rainy day activities for October half term in [location]. Would you like me to look for something else?" and stop. 5. Present the backup options to the person alongside the museums and parks.
