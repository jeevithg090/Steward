---
name: gather-free-activities
description: Search the web for free museums and parks open in October half term. List each with address and opening hours. If nothing is found, say so and ask the person for their location.
---

1. Use web_search to look for "free museums October half term" followed by the person's town or city. 2. If the person has not given a location, ask them for it before searching. 3. Scan the results for museums that list free entry. 4. Record the name, address and opening hours for each. 5. If no free museums are found, say "I could not find any free museums for October half term in [location]. Would you like me to search for paid ones or a different area?" and stop. 6. Repeat the same process for free parks, noting any opening times or seasonal closures. 7. If a search returns no results, tell the person plainly what could not be checked and ask how they would like to proceed.
