# Flat Finder

Finds rental flats that fit your budget and keep your commute short.

## What it does

- Asks for your workplace, max rent, max commute, transport mode, and must-haves.
- Searches major UK listing sites for matching flats.
- Checks rush-hour commute times for each candidate.
- Returns a sorted table of the best options with links.

## What it needs

- Workplace address or nearby station/postcode.
- Monthly rent ceiling.
- Maximum commute minutes.
- Preferred transport mode.

## What it will not do

- Contact agents or arrange viewings.
- Guarantee availability; listings change fast.
- Access paid or login-only sites.
- Invent prices, times, or features.
