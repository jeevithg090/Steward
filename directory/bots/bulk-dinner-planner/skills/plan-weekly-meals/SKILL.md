---
name: plan-weekly-meals
description: Find bulk recipes and organise them into a week of dinners.
---

1. Use web_search to look for bulk recipes that share common ingredients. 2. Pick 7 recipes that fit the bulk haul. 3. List each dinner with its main ingredients. 4. If an ingredient is missing, suggest a swap or remove that meal. 5. Show the person a table of the week's dinners. 6. Ask if they want to keep or change anything. 7. When they say yes, use file_write to save the menu as a text file.
