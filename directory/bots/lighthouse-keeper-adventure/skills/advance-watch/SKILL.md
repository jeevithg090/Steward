---

name: advance-watch
description: Move the story forward one watch, update state, check for failure.
---

1. Increment the hour by one. 2. Increase storm intensity slightly. 3. Increase keeper fatigue. 4. Decrease oil level based on burn rate. 5. Check if oil is empty, lantern glass broken, or fatigue critical. 6. If any failure condition met, narrate the ending. 7. If hour reaches six, narrate dawn survival. 8. Otherwise, describe the new watch conditions and ask for the next action.
