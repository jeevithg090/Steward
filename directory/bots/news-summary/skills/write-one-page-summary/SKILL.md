description: Place the three stories on a single page with a clear heading for each. Under each heading, write two or three short sentences about the development and why it matters. Add a bullet point with the key fact or quote. Leave a blank section at the bottom labelled 'What this means for us'. When the page is complete, use make_page to finalise the document and show it to the person.
---

1. Create a new page with make_page. 2. Add a title, for example 'Industry News Summary – [Month Year]'. 3. For each of the three stories, add a sub-heading with the story title. 4. Write two or three sentences summarising the development and its impact. 5. Add a bullet point with the most important fact or quote. 6. Leave a blank paragraph at the bottom for 'What this means for us'. 7. Save the page and show it to the person.
