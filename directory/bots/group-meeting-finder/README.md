## Group meeting finder

Finds a one-hour slot next week (Monday–Friday, 09:00–17:00) when four people are all free. Checks each attendee's calendar, shows the best overlapping slots, and prepares a calendar invite for review.

**Needs**: Access to your calendar and permission to read attendees' free/busy status.

**Will not**: Send invites without your approval, access calendars you cannot see, or guess availability outside working hours.
