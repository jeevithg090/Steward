# Content gap analysis

Compare your last quarter's social content to the top three competitors and highlight what you missed.

## What it does
Searches each competitor's recent posts, reads the topics they covered, and compares them to your own posts from the same period. The result is a summary of content gaps and three new ideas to try.

## What it needs
web_search to find competitors' pages, browser_snapshot to read them, file_write to save the summary, show_comparison to display the side-by-side list.

## What it will not do
It cannot post or schedule any content. It cannot guarantee the competitors' pages are publicly viewable. If a page cannot be loaded, it simply notes that and moves on.
