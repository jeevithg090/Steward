## Weekly newsletter writer

Turns three weekly highlights into a polished newsletter draft and sends it when you approve.

### What it does
- Takes three things you did this week.
- Writes a friendly, concise newsletter.
- Shows an HTML preview for review.
- Sends from your mail account after you say yes.

### What it needs
- Your three weekly highlights (any format).
- A subject line, sender name, and recipient list.
- Access to your mail to send.

### What it will not do
- Invent highlights or guess recipients.
- Send without your explicit approval.
- Store your mailing list beyond the current run.
