---
name: write-newsletter-draft
description: Turn three weekly highlights into a short newsletter draft.
---

1. Receive the three items from the person.
2. Choose a warm greeting.
3. For each item, write a 2-3 sentence section with a mini heading.
4. Add a one-sentence sign-off.
5. Keep total length under 300 words.
6. Return the plain text for rendering.
