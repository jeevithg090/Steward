# Capital gains estimate
Calculate your capital gains tax from stock sales using your brokerage statements.

What it does: reads your brokerage files, looks up current tax rates, and makes a table of what you likely owe.
What it needs: file_read to read your statements, web_search for current rates, show_table to display results, make_page for the summary.
What it will not do: it does not file tax returns, it does not give legal or financial advice, and it cannot access accounts it is not connected to.
