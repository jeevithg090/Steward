---
name: summarise-results
description: Turn test numbers and doctor notes into a short plain-language summary.
---

1. Identify the total cholesterol, LDL, HDL and any doctor comments from the report. 2. Write one sentence for each number saying whether it is high, low or normal for the person's age and sex. 3. List the main doctor advice, if any. 4. Keep the tone friendly and factual, no medical advice.
