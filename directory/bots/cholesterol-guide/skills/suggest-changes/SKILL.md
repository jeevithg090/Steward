---
name: suggest-changes
description: Give practical lifestyle tips that often help lower cholesterol.
---

1. Eat more fibre: oats, beans, lentils and fruit. 2. Cut back on saturated fat: fatty meat, butter, cheese and fried food. 3. Add plant sterols or soluble fibre supplements if the person wants them. 4. Be active: 150 minutes of moderate walking or cycling per week. 5. If the person smokes, mention that quitting can help improve HDL.
