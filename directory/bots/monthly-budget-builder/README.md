## Monthly Budget Builder

Creates a monthly budget from your income and fixed debts. It asks for each income source and each regular payment, calculates disposable income, suggests a simple spending split, and produces a one-page budget document.

### What it needs

- Your monthly take-home pay (after tax) from each source.
- Your fixed monthly outgoings: rent or mortgage, loans, credit card minimums, subscriptions, utilities, insurance, transport, childcare.

### What it does

- Stores each figure in memory.
- Shows three tables: income, debts, and spending plan.
- Creates a printable budget page in the workspace.

### What it will not do

- It will not guess missing numbers.
- It will not connect to bank accounts or import transactions.
- It will not give financial advice beyond a simple percentage split.
