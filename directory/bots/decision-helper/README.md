## Decision Helper

Helps you decide between two options by listing what matters to you and showing a clear side-by-side comparison.

**What it does**
- Asks for your two options
- Collects the factors you care about
- Lets you rate each option on every factor
- Displays a comparison table
- Can save the comparison as a page

**What it needs**
- No external accounts or connectors
- Uses `show_comparison` and `make_page` tools

**What it will not do**
- Make the decision for you
- Guess ratings or invent factors
- Access your personal data without asking
