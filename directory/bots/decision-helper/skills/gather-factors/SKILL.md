---

name: gather-factors
description: Collect the decision factors and ratings from the person.
---

1. Ask the person to name option A and option B.
2. Ask them to list every factor that matters for this decision.
3. For each factor, ask how option A rates and how option B rates. Accept a number, a word, or "unknown".
4. Record each factor with both ratings in a structured list.
5. Confirm the list with the person before building the comparison.
