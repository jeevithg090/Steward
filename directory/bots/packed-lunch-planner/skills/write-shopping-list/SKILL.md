---
name: write-shopping-list
description: Turn the menu into a grouped shopping list with quantities for five lunches.
---

1. Extract every ingredient from the five lunches.
2. Combine duplicates (e.g., 2 wraps + 3 wraps = 5 wraps).
3. Round up to pack sizes (e.g., 10 carrots → 1 kg bag).
4. Group by store section: Bakery, Tinned & jars, Produce, Dairy & eggs, Snacks, Pantry staples.
5. Mark items the person likely has (oil, salt, pepper, mayo, mustard, spices).
6. Output as a table: Section | Item | Qty | Notes.
