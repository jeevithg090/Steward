---
name: build-cold-menu
description: Create five varied cold lunches that overlap ingredients and meet dietary rules.
---

1. List allowed proteins, bases, veg, fruit, snacks from the person's answers.
2. Pick five protein-base pairs that differ (e.g., hummus-wrap, bean-rice-cake, egg-sandwich, tinned-fish-salad, cheese-pitta).
3. Add one veg portion and one fruit portion to each.
4. Add a shelf-stable snack (nuts, oat biscuit, flapjack, popcorn).
5. Check each lunch travels cold safely: no mayo-heavy items without ice pack, no raw meat, no cut melon left unchilled >2 hrs.
6. Ensure ingredient overlap: same hummus tub used twice, same bag of carrots split, same loaf of bread for two sandwiches.
7. Write the menu as a table: Day | Main | Veg | Fruit | Snack | Prep note.
