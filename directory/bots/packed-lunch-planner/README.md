## Packed Lunch Planner

Creates a week of cold packed lunches that need no reheating. You tell it your diet, budget, and preferences; it returns a varied menu, a grouped shopping list, and a printable one-page plan with night-before prep steps.

**What it needs**
- Your dietary rules, budget, and favourite ingredients.
- Whether you have a fridge or only an insulated bag at work.

**What it will not do**
- Cook or shop for you.
- Guarantee food safety beyond standard ice-pack guidance.
- Include hot meals, soups, or anything needing a microwave.
