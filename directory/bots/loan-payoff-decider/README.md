# Loan payoff decider

Helps you compare paying off a loan early versus saving or investing the same lump sum. It gathers your loan details, fetches current savings and low-risk investment rates, calculates the net difference over the remaining loan term, and gives you a clear comparison table and a summary page with all assumptions and sources.

## What it needs
- Loan balance, APR, remaining months, any early repayment charge.
- The lump sum you are considering.
- Access to the web to fetch current rates.

## What it will not do
- Give financial advice or a recommendation.
- Access your bank or loan accounts.
- Guarantee future rates or tax treatment.
- Handle complex tax situations (for example, higher-rate tax on savings interest).
