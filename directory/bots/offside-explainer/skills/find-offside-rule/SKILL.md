---
name: find-offside-rule
description: Locate the current official offside rule wording from a governing body.
---

1. Search for 'official offside rule Laws of the Game IFAB'.
2. Open the most recent Laws of the Game document from the International Football Association Board.
3. Extract the exact wording for Law 11 (Offside).
4. If the document cannot be reached, note that and use the widely accepted standard wording: a player is in an offside position if they are nearer to the opponents' goal line than both the ball and the second-last opponent when the ball is played to them, and they become involved in active play.
