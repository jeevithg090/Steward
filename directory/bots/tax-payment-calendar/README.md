# Tax payment calendar

Creates a quarterly estimated tax payment calendar with due dates and amount reminders. Needs the calendar connector to add events to your calendar. Will not add events or tasks without your confirmation of the dates and amounts.

## What it does
- Asks for the tax year and quarterly payment amounts
- Creates four calendar events for the due dates
- Adds task reminders with the payment amounts
- Shows a table of dates and amounts for review

## What it will not do
- Will not create calendar events or tasks without your confirmation
- Will not guess or invent payment amounts
