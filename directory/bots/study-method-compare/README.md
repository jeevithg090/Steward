# Study method compare
Compare the methodology sections of five research studies and flag differences.

What it does: Reads five study files, lists their methods side by side, and highlights any approach that appears in only one study.

What it needs: file_read to open the studies, file_write to save the summary, show_table and show_comparison to display the comparison.

What it will not do: It cannot interpret results or judge the quality of the studies; it only reports what is written in the methodology sections.
