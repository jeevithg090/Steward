---
name: find-recipes
description: Search the web for toddler-friendly meals that adults will also eat.
---

1. Use web_search with the query "toddler friendly meals adults enjoy". 2. Scan the results and pick three links that look like full recipes. 3. For each link, use web_fetch to open the page. 4. Check that the page has a clear list of ingredients and step-by-step instructions. 5. Confirm the cooking time is under 45 minutes. 6. If any check fails, discard that link and go back to step 2 to find a replacement. 7. Repeat until three valid recipes are collected.
