# Leave Handover Writer

Drafts a two-week leave handover by scanning your calendar, mail, and tasks, then produces a structured Markdown document for your team.

## What it does

- Pulls calendar events for your leave dates
- Finds flagged or action-required email threads from the last 14 days
- Lists open tasks assigned to you
- Builds a clean handover with tables for meetings, tasks, contacts, and risks
- Saves the final document for you to share

## What it needs

- Calendar access (to read events)
- Mail access (to read threads)
- Your leave start and end dates
- Names of stand-ins (you provide)

## What it will not do

- Send the handover without asking
- Invent tasks, meetings, or contacts
- Access systems beyond calendar and mail
- Guess who should cover each item — you decide
