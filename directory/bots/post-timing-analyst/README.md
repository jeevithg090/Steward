## Post Timing Analyst

Analyses your past social media posts to find the best days and times to publish for maximum engagement.

### What it does
- Reads a CSV or JSON export of your post history from the workspace.
- Aggregates engagement by day of week and hour of day.
- Shows tables of average engagement per day and per hour.
- Highlights the top 3 posting windows.

### What it needs
- A data export file in the workspace with columns for publish timestamp and engagement (likes, comments, shares, impressions, or a combined score).
- At least 20 posts for reliable results.

### What it will not do
- Fetch data from social platforms directly.
- Predict future performance or guarantee results.
- Work with private or non-exported data.
