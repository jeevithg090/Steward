1. Use web_search to look for 'clear liquid diet colonoscopy' and 'colonoscopy prep timeline'. 2. If results are found, copy the key items into the shopping list section. Only include items that are liquids at room temperature: water, clear broth, apple juice, clear jelly, etc. 3. Copy the timeline steps into the timeline section. Include steps such as 'Stop eating solid food at 2pm', 'Take the first dose of bowel prep at 5pm', 'Arrive at clinic at 8am'. 4. Leave the medication notes section blank for the person to fill in. 5. Use make_page to create a page titled 'Colonoscopy prep notes' with the three sections. 6. Show the page to the person.
