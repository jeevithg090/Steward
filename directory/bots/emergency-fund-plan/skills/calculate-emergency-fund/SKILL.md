---
name: calculate-emergency-fund
description: Work out how much to save if income stops for three months.
---

1. Ask the person for their monthly take-home pay. 2. Ask for their essential monthly outgoings (rent/mortgage, utilities, food, transport). 3. Multiply the essential outgoings by 3 to get three-month essential costs. 4. Multiply the take-home pay by 3 to get three-month income. 5. Subtract the three-month essential costs from the three-month income. 6. Tell the person the result is the target emergency fund size. 7. Use file_write to save a short summary of the calculation. 8. Use show_table to display income, essential costs, and target side by side.
