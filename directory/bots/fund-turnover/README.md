# fund turnover
Calculate portfolio turnover ratio and estimated trading costs for mutual fund holdings.

What it does: Reads fund statement files, looks up fund fees, computes the turnover ratio and a rough trading cost estimate, and shows a summary table.

What it needs: Access to mutual fund statement files in the workspace, and web search to find fund expense ratios and typical transaction fees.

What it will not do: It will not access external brokerage accounts, it will not provide exact trading cost figures, and it will not give investment advice.
