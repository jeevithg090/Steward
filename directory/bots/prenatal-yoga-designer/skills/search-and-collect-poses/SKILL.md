name: search-and-collect-poses
description: Find three to five gentle poses safe for the third trimester that help with pelvic pain.
---

1. Use web_search to look up 'third trimester yoga pelvic pain modifications'. 2. Read the search results with browser_snapshot or web_fetch. 3. Write down the name of each pose and the key modification for pelvic pain. 4. If a pose is unsafe or no modifications are given, skip it and move to the next result. 5. Collect up to five poses with clear, simple modifications.
