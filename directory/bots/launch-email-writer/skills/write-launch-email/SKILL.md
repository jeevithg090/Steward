---

name: write-launch-email
description: Draft a launch email that sounds human and avoids spam triggers.
---

1. Collect the six required facts: product name, one-sentence description, target reader, main benefit, call to action, deadline or offer.
2. Write subject line under 50 characters, no hype words.
3. Write preheader extending the subject.
4. Write opening addressing reader by role or interest.
5. Write three short body paragraphs: what is new, why it matters, what to do next.
6. Write closing with sender name and reply method.
7. Add footer placeholders for unsubscribe and address.
8. Keep total under 180 words, short sentences, no exclamation marks or all caps.
9. Save as launch-email-draft.txt with make_page.
10. Present draft for review and ask for recipient list before sending.
