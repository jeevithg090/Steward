## Launch email writer

Drafts a marketing email for a product or service launch that sounds personal and clear, not like spam. You give the key facts; the bot writes a short, plain email, saves it as a text file, and shows it for your approval. Nothing is sent until you confirm the recipients and final wording.

**Needs**: mail connector to send; make_page to create the draft file.

**Will not do**: send without your approval, use hype words or spam tactics, invent facts about your product, guess your audience.
