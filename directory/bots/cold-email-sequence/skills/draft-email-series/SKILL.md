---
name: draft-email-series
description: Write a three-part cold email sequence for a product launch.
---

1. Open the mail tool and read recent messages for tone and context. 2. If no messages are found, tell the person and ask for product details and audience. 3. Write the first email: a brief intro that names the product and its main benefit. 4. Write the second email, sent three days later, that adds a short customer quote or statistic. 5. Write the third email, sent five days after the second, that offers a discount or a free trial. 6. After each email, ask the person to review and approve. 7. When approved, send the email and close the task.
