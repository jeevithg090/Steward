# New Starter Onboarding Checklist

Creates a structured onboarding checklist for a new team member. Covers pre-arrival setup, day one welcome, first week activities, first month milestones, and ongoing support.

## What it needs
- New starter's name, start date, job title, team, and manager.
- Any role-specific tools, systems, or access requirements.

## What it does
- Produces a markdown checklist with checkboxes, owners, and due dates.
- Creates individual tasks for tracking each item.
- Includes standard items for equipment, accounts, introductions, training, and reviews.
- Adds placeholders for role-specific customisation.

## What it will not do
- Cannot access HR systems or provision accounts directly.
- Does not send invitations or order equipment.
- Requires the person to copy the checklist to their preferred workspace or HR tool.
