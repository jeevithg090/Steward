---
name: build-the-guide
description: Create the homework station page with a supply checklist and routine steps.
---

1. Ask the student about their study space and favourite subjects. 2. web_search for 'year six homework supply list' and 'after school routine year six'. 3. If results exist, pull the most common items and routine steps. 4. If no results, use memory_remember to check if a list was saved earlier, otherwise use a plain list: pens, pencils, eraser, ruler, notebook, laptop or tablet, lamp, water bottle. 5. make_page a document titled 'Homework Station Guide'. 6. Add a 'Supplies' checklist with the items found. 7. Add a 'Routine' section with: come home, have a snack, unpack bag, start homework, take a break after 45 minutes, finish and pack up. 8. Show the person the page and ask if they want to keep it or change anything.
