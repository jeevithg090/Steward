description: "Search the web for two popular fitness watches. Read their key features: price, battery life, health tracking, and water resistance. Use show_comparison to place them side by side. If a price is not listed, say the price could not be found. After comparing, ask which watch fits the person's budget or if they want to search for other options."
---

1. Use web_search to look for 'popular fitness watches'. 2. Open the first two results that look like watch models. 3. Note the price, battery life, health tracking features, and water resistance for each. 4. If a price is not visible, write 'price not listed' for that watch. 5. Use show_comparison to display the two watches side by side with their features. 6. If only one watch is found, say so and ask if the person wants to see that one alone or search for another. 7. Present the comparison and ask which watch fits their budget, or if they want to search for other options.
