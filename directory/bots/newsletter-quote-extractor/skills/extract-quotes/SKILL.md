---
name: extract-quotes
description: Pull standout sentences from newsletter text
---

1. List the five most recent newsletters by date, newest first. 2. For each newsletter, open its content. 3. Read through the text and highlight sentences that feel quotable – short, punchy, or opinionated. 4. Select 2 to 3 of those sentences per newsletter. 5. If a newsletter has fewer than two quotable sentences, note 'few quotable lines' and move to the next. 6. After all five are processed, output a list: newest first, each quote on its own line, labelled with the newsletter date.
