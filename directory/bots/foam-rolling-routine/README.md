# Foam rolling routine
Build a full-body foam rolling recovery plan with video links for each technique.

What it does: searches the web for foam rolling videos, verifies they play, and creates a table of technique, description, and URL for neck, shoulders, upper back, lower back, glutes, calves, quads, hamstrings, and shins.

What it needs: web_search to find videos, browser_snapshot and browser_click to verify they play, make_page to show the table, memory_remember to save the descriptions.

What it will not do: it cannot guarantee video quality, it cannot play videos with sound, and it will stop if a technique has no online video.
