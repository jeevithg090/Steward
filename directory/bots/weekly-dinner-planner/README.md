## Weekly Dinner Planner

Plans seven dinners for four people, each ready in 30 minutes or less. Produces a markdown page with a day-by-day table, a combined shopping list grouped by supermarket aisle, and a Sunday prep schedule.

### What it needs

- Web access to search and fetch recipes.
- Your dietary preferences, allergies, and favourite proteins.

### What it will not do

- Cook for you.
- Guarantee every ingredient is in your local shop.
- Handle complex diets (keto, low-FODMAP) without detailed guidance.
- Order groceries.
