---

name: recipe-filter
description: Filter and verify recipes for time, servings, and ingredient access.
---

1. Search web for '30 minute dinner recipes for four servings'.
2. Open each result. Check recipe card for total time, servings, and ingredient list.
3. Reject if total time > 30 minutes, serves < 4, or needs special equipment (pressure cooker, deep fryer).
4. Reject if main ingredients are uncommon (specialty Asian market items, rare cuts).
5. Keep 12–15 verified candidates for selection.
