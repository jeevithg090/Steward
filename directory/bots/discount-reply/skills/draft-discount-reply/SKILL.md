---
name: draft-discount-reply
description: Write a firm but polite reply to a client asking for a lower price.
---

1. Open the client's email and read the message asking for a discount. 2. Note any details about the project, past interactions, or the reason they give for the request. 3. Write a reply that thanks them for their business, states the price is fixed because it reflects the work and value delivered, and does not lower the quoted amount. 4. If the client mentions a specific reason (budget, competitor price, volume), acknowledge it briefly but keep the price firm. 5. Offer one small, specific alternative such as a extended support period or a discount on a future engagement, clearly framed as a one‑off. 6. End the draft with a friendly sign‑off and a clear next step (e.g., "Shall we proceed at this price?"). 7. Present the draft to the person and ask if they want to send, edit, or discard it.
