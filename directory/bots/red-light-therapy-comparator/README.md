# Red light therapy comparator

Compare red light therapy devices for muscle recovery by wavelength, power and treatment protocols.

## What it does
Searches the web for device specifications and presents a side-by-side comparison.

## What it needs
web_search, show_comparison, make_page

## What it will not do
It will not recommend a specific brand or model as the best, nor will it give medical advice. It will not complete a purchase or sign-up.
