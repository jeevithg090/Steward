description: Write the four preservation method sections on the page, one after another, using plain British English, short sentences and clear headings.
---

1. Create heading 'Drying herbs'. Write: 'Tie herb stems in small bunches. Hang them in a warm, airy place out of direct sunlight. When the leaves crumble easily, rub them off and store whole in airtight jars.' 2. Create heading 'Freezing herbs'. Write: 'Chop the herbs. Mix with a little water or oil. Spoon into ice cube trays. Freeze until solid. Transfer cubes to a labelled freezer bag.' 3. Create heading 'Herb oil'. Write: 'Pack clean, dry herb leaves into a jar. Cover completely with a neutral oil. Seal and store in the fridge. Use within one month.' 4. Create heading 'Herb vinegar'. Write: 'Pack clean, dry herb leaves into a jar. Cover with warm vinegar. Seal and store in a cool, dark place for two weeks. Strain and rebottle.'
