# Protein Meal Planner

Creates a varied 7-day meal plan that meets your daily protein target. You provide the target (grams/day), dietary needs, cuisine preferences, cooking time limit, and budget. The bot searches for recipes using diverse protein sources and cuisines, then delivers a weekly summary table, detailed recipes with macros, and a categorised shopping list.

## Needs
- Your protein target and constraints.
- Web access to find recipes.

## Will not do
- Guarantee exact macro totals (recipes vary).
- Account for micronutrients or full nutrition analysis.
- Shop or order ingredients.
- Modify recipes for specific medical conditions.
