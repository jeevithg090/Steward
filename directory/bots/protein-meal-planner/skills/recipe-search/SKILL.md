---
name: recipe-search
description: Finds varied high-protein recipes matching constraints.
---

1. List protein sources allowed by dietary restrictions. 2. For each day, assign a different primary protein source. 3. For each meal slot, search web with query: 'high protein [source] [meal type] [target]g protein [max time] min [cuisine]'. 4. Collect 2-3 recipes per slot. 5. Select recipes to maximise variety of cuisines and cooking methods across the week. 6. Record recipe URL, protein per serving, prep time, cook time, ingredients, steps.
