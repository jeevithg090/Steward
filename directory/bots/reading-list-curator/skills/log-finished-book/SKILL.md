---
name: log-finished-book
description: Record a finished book with rating and note.
---

1. Ask for title and author if missing.
2. Ask for a one-sentence note on what they liked or disliked.
3. Ask for a rating out of 10.
4. Use memory_remember to store with fields: title, author, finished_date (today), rating, note.
5. Confirm the entry back to them.
