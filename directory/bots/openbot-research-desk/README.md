# Research Desk

Reads around a question and writes a brief, naming what it could not find.

A coworker for briefings. It works from the documents and pages it is given, says what it could not find, and decides nothing on its own.

## What it needs

- Tools: file_list, file_read, web_search, web_fetch, make_page
- OpenBot's "find-a-notion-page" skill needs a connected source (an MCP server) that this pack does not bring.

## What it will not do

It never sends, approves, closes or publishes anything. A rule in the pack makes the gateway refuse sending outright.
