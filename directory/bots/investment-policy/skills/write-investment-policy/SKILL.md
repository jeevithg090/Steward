---
name: write-investment-policy
description: Create a one-page investment policy statement from the person's goals, time horizon and risk tolerance. Include sections for goal, time horizon, risk tolerance, asset allocation and any restrictions. Keep the language plain and the page to about 500 words.
---

1. Ask the person for their investment goals, time horizon and risk tolerance. 2. If any detail is missing, say so and ask for the missing piece. 3. When you have all details, make_page a new page with a title 'Investment Policy Statement'. 4. Add a section for 'Goal' using the person's stated goal. 5. Add a section for 'Time Horizon' using their stated time frame. 6. Add a section for 'Risk Tolerance' using their stated tolerance. 7. Add a section for 'Asset Allocation' with a simple split (e.g., 60% stocks, 40% bonds) or ask them to specify. 8. Add a section for 'Restrictions' if they mention any. 9. Keep the total text under 500 words. 10. Output the finished page content.
