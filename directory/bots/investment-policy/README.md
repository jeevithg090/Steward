# Investment policy
Writes a one-page investment policy statement from your goals and risk tolerance.

What it does: asks for your goals, time horizon and risk tolerance; creates a plain-page document; saves it as investment_policy.md.

What it needs: memory_remember to store your answers; make_page to build the page; file_write to save it.

What it will not do: it cannot give financial advice, pick specific investments or check market prices.
