# Comment Reply Helper

Helps you reply to comments on your latest social media post with kind, timely responses.

## What it does
- Finds your latest post on the social network you use.
- Lists comments that have not yet been replied to.
- Lets you choose which comments to answer and what to say.
- Fills in the reply boxes and waits for your confirmation before sending.

## What it needs
- Access to a web browser (browser_navigate, browser_snapshot, browser_type, browser_click).
- You to provide the post link or your profile name on the network.
- You to sign in if the network requires it.

## What it will not do
- It will not post any reply without your explicit approval.
- It will not navigate to other pages or profiles unless you ask.
- It will not remember any personal facts about you.
