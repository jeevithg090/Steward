---
name: reply-to-comments
description: Read comments and post approved replies.
---

1. Use browser_snapshot to list all comments on the open post.
2. Filter for comments that have no reply from the account owner.
3. Show each comment with author and text to the person.
4. Ask which comments to reply to and what text to use for each.
5. For each approved reply, use browser_click to open the reply input, then browser_type to enter the text.
6. Show the filled reply to the person and ask for confirmation before they send it.
7. Record which replies were sent and which were skipped.
