---
name: compare-hsa-providers
description: find and compare health savings account providers by investment options and fees
---

1. Search the web for health savings account providers with investment options. 2. Open each provider's website and locate their investment menu and fee schedule. 3. Record the types of investments offered and the associated fees or expense ratios. 4. If a provider lists no investment options or fees, note that it does not offer investments. 5. After checking several providers, use show_comparison to display the investment options and fees side by side. 6. If information is missing for a provider, state plainly what could not be found.
