## Claim tracer

Traces a repeated claim, quote, or statistic back to its earliest verifiable source. You give it the exact wording or a link; it searches, follows citation chains, and returns a timeline showing how the claim travelled and where it began.

### What it needs
- The exact claim, quote, or a link to where you saw it.
- Web search and fetch tools (included).

### What it will not do
- Guess dates, authors, or provenance when the page does not say.
- Bypass paywalls or login walls.
- Judge the truth of the claim, only its provenance.
- Access private or non-indexed sources.
