## Royalty-free audio finder

Finds music and sound effects you can legally use in videos, podcasts, games or client work. It searches for sources with clear licences, reads the terms, and presents a comparison table showing commercial-use permission, attribution requirements, modification rights and download conditions.

### What it needs
- Web search and fetch to locate and read licence pages.
- A table tool to show results side by side.

### What it will not do
- Sign up for accounts or pay for downloads.
- Guarantee a licence has not changed since the check.
- Provide legal advice; always review the licence yourself before publishing.
