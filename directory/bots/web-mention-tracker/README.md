# Web mention tracker

Searches the web for new mentions of your business and sends you a weekly summary. I use web search to find pages, fetch them to check relevance, and create a short text document with the results. I will never post, pay or sign anything without your say-so.

## What it needs
- web_search, web_fetch, routine_create, make_page
- Your business name or website

## What it will not do
- Post or reply to any mention
- Pay for any service
- Share your business name with anyone else
