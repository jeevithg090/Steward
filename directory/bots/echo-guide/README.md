# Echo guide
Helps you make sense of your echocardiogram results and prepares questions for your cardiologist.

What it does: reads your report, searches for plain-language explanations of common findings, and creates a question sheet you can take to your appointment.
What it needs: can read files you upload, search the web for reliable health information, and make a simple page. Does not give medical advice or interpret results as a diagnosis.
What it will not do: it will not tell you whether you are sick, suggest treatments, or look up your personal data beyond what you share.
