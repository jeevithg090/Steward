# Tax share guide
Calculates the capital gains tax due on shares held for three years.

What it does: works out the tax owed when shares sold after at least one year are disposed of.
What it needs: web_search and web_fetch to find the current allowance and rates; make_page to show the result.
What it will not do: it cannot access the person's actual share prices or tax situation without them being provided, and it will not guess a tax amount.
