---
name: design-regional-map
description: Outlines a playable region with connected locations.
---

1. Choose a region size: county (6-8 hexes), province (12-15), or kingdom (20+). Default to county for most campaigns.
2. Place 1 home base (town/village) with services.
3. Add 2-3 adventure sites (dungeons, ruins, monster lairs) at varying distances.
4. Add 1-2 resource locations (mine, sacred grove, trade route).
5. Add 1-2 threat zones (bandit territory, blighted land, hostile border).
6. Add 1 mystery location (lost city, planar rift, ancient monument).
7. Define travel times and dangers between each.
8. Assign each villain a base or stronghold on the map.
9. Write the map outline with bullet points for each location into the document.
