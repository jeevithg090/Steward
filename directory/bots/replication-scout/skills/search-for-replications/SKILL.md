---
name: search-for-replications
description: look for studies that repeat the paper's experiments
---

1. use web_search with the paper title and the word "replication". 2. scan the first page of results with browser_snapshot. 3. pick results that are peer‑reviewed repeats or reanalyses of the same experiments. 4. copy the title, authors, venue and URL into a table. 5. if the results are not studies, try a second search with "reproducible study" or the experiment name.
