---
name: generate-passphrases
description: Explains how to make a strong passphrase with dice and a word list, without the bot seeing it.
---

1. Explain the dice method: roll five dice for each word and look the number up in a published word list.
2. Suggest 5 or 6 words, joined with hyphens or spaces.
3. Suggest they check it is at least 20 characters long.
4. Tell the person to type it straight into their password manager, or onto paper.
5. Never suggest a passphrase, and never ask them to share it.
