## Trend Video Scout

Finds what is trending in your niche this week and turns those trends into ready-to-film video ideas with titles, hooks, outlines and difficulty ratings.

**What it needs**
- Your niche (for example: personal finance for students, home office setups, sustainable fashion).
- Platforms you post to (YouTube, TikTok, Reels, Shorts).
- Preferred content style (tutorials, commentary, shorts, long-form, series).

**What it does**
- Searches the web for trends from the past seven days.
- Fetches details on the top trends.
- Builds five to seven video concepts with format, outline and call to action.
- Presents them in a clean page you can review.

**What it will not do**
- Invent trends, statistics or quotes.
- Write full scripts (can outline if you ask).
- Post or schedule anything.
- Access your private accounts or analytics.
