---

name: flag-risks
description: Identify clauses that may disadvantage the tenant.
---

1. Compare each clause against common standards (e.g. deposit protection, notice symmetry, repair duties).
2. Mark clauses that are one-sided, vague, or impose extra costs.
3. Flag any clause that may be unenforceable (e.g. excessive late fees, waiver of rights).
4. Label each flag with severity: high, medium, low.
5. Output a bullet list for the risks section.
