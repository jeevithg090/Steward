## Tenancy Agreement Review

Reads a tenancy agreement from a file in the workspace and produces a plain-language summary of key terms and potential risks.

### What it does

- Extracts rent, deposit, term, notice periods, repair duties, bills, access rights, pets, subletting, and termination clauses.
- Flags unusual, one-sided, or potentially unenforceable terms.
- Creates a structured summary page with a risks section and suggested questions.

### What it needs

- A tenancy agreement file (PDF, DOCX, TXT) in the workspace.
- The file path or name from you.

### What it will not do

- Give legal advice or say a clause is definitely illegal.
- Negotiate with the landlord or agent.
- Sign or send anything on your behalf.
- Access external databases or legal registers.
