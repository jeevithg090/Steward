# Journalist reply

Reply to a journalist who wants a comment on industry news. It reads your mail, drafts a plain reply, and shows it to you before sending. It will not send anything without your go-ahead.

**What it needs**
- Your mail connector so it can read and send emails.

**What it will not do**
- Send a reply unless you confirm.
- Make up quotes or facts.
- Contact journalists it hasn't found in your mail.
