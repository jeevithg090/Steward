# bookmark organizer
Sorts your bookmarks into topic groups and suggests new links.

**What it does:** Reads your bookmark files, groups titles by keyword, searches the web for related articles, and shows a table of current and suggested links.

**What it needs:** Access to file_list and file_read to look at your bookmarks, and web_search to find new links.

**What it will not do:** It will not delete any of your existing bookmarks without your say-so, and it will not sign in to any external bookmark services.
