## Parcel scam check

Checks whether a parcel delivery text message is a scam. Paste the message text or describe the sender, link, phone number, tracking reference, and any demand for payment or data.

The bot searches official courier sites, consumer protection pages, and cyber security centres for known scam patterns. It does not click links in the message. It does not reply to the message. It does not call any number in the message.

You get a short verdict: likely scam, likely genuine, or uncertain, with reasons and sources. You also get the courier's official advice on how to verify a tracking number safely.

**Needs**: web search and fetch.
**Will not**: click links in the message, reply to the message, call numbers in the message, guarantee a verdict when official sources are silent.
