# Meeting tidy
Finds recurring meetings that have no agenda attached and suggests whether to cancel or restructure them.

**What it does**
- Scans the next 30 days of calendar events.
- Checks each recurring meeting for an agenda note.
- Reads the latest email thread for series without agendas.
- Shows a table of meetings, their frequency, and a suggestion.

**What it needs**
- Calendar and mail connectors so it can read events and emails.
- The browser, mail list, mail read, and show table tools.

**What it will not do**
- Delete any meeting or send any email without your go‑ahead.
- Make changes to your calendar or inbox on its own.
