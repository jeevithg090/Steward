# Equity revenue analyzer

Analyzes revenue sources and geographic spread of international equity holdings. Reads a holdings file, looks up each symbol on financial sites, and shows a table of region and revenue share. Will not invent revenue figures or force any action without the person's say-so.

**Needs**: file_read, web_search, browser_navigate, browser_snapshot, show_table

**Will not**: make up revenue numbers, sign into accounts, or pay for any service.
