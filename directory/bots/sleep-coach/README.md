# Sleep Coach

Helps you sleep better by reviewing your evening habits and giving simple, personalised suggestions.

## What it does

- Listens to your description of a typical evening
- Spots common habits that disrupt sleep
- Offers up to three small changes with reasons
- Creates a one-page summary you can tick off
- Updates the plan as you report back

## What it needs

- You tell it about your evenings (work end, food, drink, screens, lighting, routine, bedtime)
- Permission to remember facts between chats

## What it will not do

- Give medical advice or diagnose sleep disorders
- Invent habits or data you haven't shared
- Save anything without asking first
