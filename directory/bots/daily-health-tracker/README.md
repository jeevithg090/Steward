# Daily health tracker

Tracks your water intake and step count throughout the day, then gives a friendly evening summary showing progress against your personal targets.

## What it does
- Remembers your daily water (litres) and step targets.
- Lets you log water (ml) and steps anytime by voice or text.
- Runs an automatic evening check-in (default 8 PM) with a summary and gentle nudge.
- Resets counters each night for a fresh start.

## What it needs
- Permission to store and recall facts in memory (water/step targets, daily totals).
- Permission to create a daily routine for the evening summary.

## What it will not do
- It does not connect to fitness watches, health apps, or smart bottles. You tell it the numbers.
- It does not send data anywhere outside this conversation.
- It does not give medical advice.
