---
name: check-targets
description: Verify that each day's chosen recipes meet the daily olive oil and fish minimums.
---

1. For each of the 7 days, add the olive oil grams from the three meals. 2. Add the fish grams from the three meals. 3. Compare the totals to the person's targets. 4. If any day is below the olive oil minimum, find one extra recipe for that day's meal and add its amount. 5. If any day is below the fish minimum, find a fish-based recipe for that day's meal and add its portion. 6. Repeat the check until all 7 days meet both targets.
