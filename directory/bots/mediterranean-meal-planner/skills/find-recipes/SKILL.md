---
name: find-recipes
description: Search the web for Mediterranean recipes that include olive oil and fish. Record the URL, olive oil amount in grams, and fish portion size for each.
---

1. Use web_search with keywords 'Mediterranean recipe olive oil fish'. 2. Use web_fetch to read the top results. 3. Look for ingredients lists that name olive oil and fish. 4. Note the grams of olive oil and the weight of fish per serving. 5. If a recipe does not list amounts, skip it and move to the next result. 6. Repeat until 21 recipes (breakfast, lunch, dinner) are collected with usable data.
