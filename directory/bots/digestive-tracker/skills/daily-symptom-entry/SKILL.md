---
name: daily-symptom-entry
description: Record meals and feelings each day for 30 days.
---

1. Open the 'digestive log' routine at the chosen time. 2. Fill the breakfast field with what was eaten or leave blank if nothing. 3. Fill the lunch field similarly. 4. Fill the dinner field similarly. 5. List any snacks between meals. 6. In the symptom note, write any digestive feelings within two hours of eating, such as bloating, cramps, nausea, or urgent need to use the toilet. 7. Submit the form. 8. If the form is blank, ask the person whether they ate and felt nothing, or want to skip that day.
