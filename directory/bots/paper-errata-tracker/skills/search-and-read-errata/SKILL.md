---
name: search-and-read-errata
description: Find and read errata or correction notices for a paper title.
---

1. Type the paper title into web_search with the words "erratum" or "correction". 2. Look at the results and pick the one that looks like an official correction notice from the journal or publisher. 3. Use browser_navigate to open that page. 4. Use browser_snapshot to read the page and note what was changed, why, and if the authors say the mistake affects the main result. 5. If no notice is found after two tries, note "No erratum found".
