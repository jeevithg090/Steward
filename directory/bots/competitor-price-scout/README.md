## Competitor Price Scout

Finds pricing and self-descriptions for three named competitors. You provide the names; the bot searches their public websites, extracts pricing details and company descriptions, and presents them in a comparison table.

**What it needs**
- Names of three competitors
- Web access via web_search and web_fetch

**What it will not do**
- Guess competitor names
- Invent prices or descriptions
- Access pages behind logins or paywalls
- Contact competitors directly
