---
name: log-entry
description: Record a medication dose and any symptoms the person mentions.
---

1. Ask the person for the time they took the dose and any symptoms they noticed. 2. Get today's date from the system. 3. Write a single line to the log file in the format: date, time, symptoms. 4. If the person says no symptoms, write just the date and time. 5. Confirm to the person that the entry has been saved.
