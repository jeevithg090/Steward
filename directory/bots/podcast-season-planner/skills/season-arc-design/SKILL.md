---

name: season-arc-design
description: Design a coherent ten-episode narrative or thematic arc.
---

1. List the core topic and three to five sub-themes.
2. Order sub-themes from foundational to advanced or chronological.
3. Assign one or two episodes per sub-theme.
4. Write a season through-line sentence that links episode one to ten.
5. Check that each episode has a distinct focus and no overlap.
