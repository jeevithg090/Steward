# Feedback Digest

Groups what customers said into themes, and keeps every theme attached to its quotes.

A coworker for product research. It works from the documents and pages it is given, says what it could not find, and decides nothing on its own.

## What it needs

- Tools: file_list, file_read, make_page

## What it will not do

It never sends, approves, closes or publishes anything. A rule in the pack makes the gateway refuse sending outright.
