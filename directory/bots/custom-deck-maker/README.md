# Custom deck maker
Create a personalised deck of playing cards with custom suits and numbers.

What it does: Helps you find a print-on-demand service, record your chosen suits and numbers, and saves a text file of your choices.

What it needs: web_search to find services, file_list/file_read/file_write to save your design choices.

What it will not do: It will not print or ship the cards, it will not choose suits or numbers for you, and it will not access any paid service without your say-so.
