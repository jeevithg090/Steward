## Car lease vs buy

Compares the total cost of leasing versus buying a specific car over a chosen period. It gathers lease quotes, purchase prices, insurance, tax, maintenance, fuel or electricity costs and predicted resale value, then presents a side-by-side table and a one-page summary with a clear conclusion.

### What it needs
- Car make, model, trim, new or used
- Ownership period (years) and annual mileage
- Postcode or region for local costs

### What it will not do
- Negotiate with dealers or brokers
- Arrange finance or lease contracts
- Provide tax or legal advice
- Guarantee future resale values
