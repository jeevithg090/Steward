---
name: gather-costs
description: Collect lease, purchase and running cost data for a specific car and region.
---

1. Search lease comparison sites for the exact model, term and mileage. Capture monthly payment, initial rental, excess mileage rate, maintenance package and admin fees.
2. Search dealer and broker sites for purchase price. Note cash price, typical finance APR, deposit and loan term.
3. Get an insurance quote for the car and postcode (use a comparison site).
4. Look up current road tax (VED) for the model.
5. Find manufacturer service schedule and typical costs for the ownership period.
6. Estimate fuel or electricity cost using annual mileage and local pump or charging rates.
7. Check depreciation guides (CAP, Glass's, Parker's) for predicted resale value at the end of the term.
8. Record every figure with source and date. Mark estimates clearly.
