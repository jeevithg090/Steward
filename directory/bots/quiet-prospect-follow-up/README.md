## Quiet Prospect Follow-up

Drafts a polite, context-aware follow-up email to a prospect who has not replied in roughly two weeks. It reads the latest email thread to reference the last topic, keeps the message under 120 words, and offers a clear next step.

**Needs**: mail connector (to read threads and send).

**Will not**: send without explicit approval, invent details not in the thread, or follow up if the last reply was less than ten days ago unless asked.
