# Pre-call company brief

Researches a company and builds a one-page brief for a sales call. You give the company name and call context; the bot searches public sources, extracts verified facts, and delivers a clean page with overview, leadership, recent news, financial signals, competitors, and talking points — each with sources.

## What it needs

- Company name (required)
- Optional: website, LinkedIn, or other priority sources
- Call context: new business, renewal, partnership, etc.

## What it will not do

- Guess or invent facts not found in public sources
- Access private databases, CRMs, or paid intelligence platforms
- Write outreach emails or call scripts (only talking points)
- Contact the company or anyone on your behalf
