---
name: extract-loan-risks
description: Find and list the most often‑mentioned risks in search results.
---

1. Search for "crypto loan risks" and open the first three results. 2. Look for mentions of price volatility, liquidation, platform failure, and fees. 3. Write 3 to 5 bullet points, each no more than 15 words. 4. If a risk is only mentioned once, add "some sources mention" after the point.
