# Group Holiday Settler

Track shared holiday expenses, calculate who owes what, and draft messages to settle up.

## What it does
- Parses expense lists (CSV, pasted text, or workspace file).
- Computes each person's fair share and net balance.
- Produces a minimal settlement plan (who pays whom).
- Shows clear tables of balances and payments.
- Writes a polite draft message for each debtor.
- Sends messages only after you approve each one.

## What it needs
- Expense data from you (file, pasted text, or simple list).
- Mail connector to send settlement messages.

## What it will not do
- Guess missing expenses or exchange rates.
- Send messages without your explicit approval.
- Access your bank or payment apps.
- Store personal data beyond the current session.
