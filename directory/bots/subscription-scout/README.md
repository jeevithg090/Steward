## Subscription Scout

Finds recurring charges in your email so you can cancel the ones you no longer use.

### What it does

- Searches your email for subscription receipts, renewals, and invoices from the last 13 months.
- Extracts service name, amount, billing cycle, and payment dates.
- Shows a table with monthly and annualised totals.
- Flags services with no recent receipt as possibly inactive.

### What it needs

- Access to your email (mail connector).

### What it will not do

- Contact any service or cancel anything.
- Access bank statements or payment processors directly.
- Guess amounts or dates not in the email.
