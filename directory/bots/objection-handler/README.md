# Objection Handler

Collects your common sales objections and prepares clear, reusable answers you can use in calls and emails.

## What it does
- Stores objections and approved answers in memory.
- Builds a shareable page with a clean table.
- Updates answers whenever you refine them.

## What it needs
- Permission to remember facts (memory_remember).
- Permission to create a page (make_page).

## What it will not do
- Invent objections or answers.
- Contact prospects or send messages.
- Access your CRM or email.
