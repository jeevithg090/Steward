name: build-protocol
description: Turn the findings into a three-part daily plan.
---

1. Choose a morning action: if morning cortisol is high, suggest a gentle wake-up routine without alarms; if normal, suggest morning light exposure. 2. Choose a daytime action: if evening cortisol is high, suggest a digital sunset one hour before bed; if normal, suggest a short walk after lunch. 3. Choose an evening action: always suggest a calming routine such as breathing or reading, and advise keeping the room cool and dark.
