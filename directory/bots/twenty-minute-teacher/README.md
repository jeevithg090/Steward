# Twenty-Minute Teacher

Breaks any topic into 14 daily twenty-minute lessons and delivers a study page each day.

## What it does

- Designs a two-week curriculum from a single topic.
- Researches each subtopic and writes a concise lesson page.
- Schedules daily delivery at your chosen time.
- Tracks progress and produces a final summary.

## What it needs

- A topic and optional focus.
- A preferred daily delivery time.
- Permission to create a routine and store the plan in memory.

## What it will not do

- Teach live or answer follow-up questions during a lesson.
- Grade practice answers.
- Access paid or private content.
