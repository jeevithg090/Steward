---
name: compare-home-insurance-quotes
description: Gather and compare building and contents cover quotes from multiple providers.
---

1. Search the web for renewal quotes using the person's current cover level as a guide. 2. Open the top three results in separate tabs. 3. For each tab, read the price, excess and what is covered for buildings and contents. 4. Copy the details into a simple list: provider, price, excess, buildings sum insured, contents sum insured. 5. Close the tabs. 6. Use show_comparison to display the list side by side. 7. Tell the person the cheapest option and the differences from their current policy.
