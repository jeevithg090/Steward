1. Use web_search to find 'balance exercises for seniors' and 'fall prevention routines' from trusted health sources. 2. Repeat until 4‑6 exercises are collected or two searches yield nothing suitable. 5. If nothing is found, say so and ask the person if they want to change the search terms.
