## Five-a-side league finder

Finds local five-a-side football leagues that have open spots for individual players. You give a postcode or town and say whether you prefer indoor or outdoor. The bot searches league websites, council sports pages and venue sites for current vacancies, then returns a short list with venue, night, cost and sign-up details.

**Needs**: web search and browsing tools.

**Will not**: create accounts, pay fees, or contact leagues on your behalf.
