## Team Morning Brief

Delivers a daily morning summary of your favourite team's last result and next fixture.

### What it does

- Remembers your team once you tell it.
- Each morning, searches for the most recent match (within ~48 hours) and the next scheduled fixture.
- Returns a three-line brief: team, last result, next fixture.

### What it needs

- Web search and fetch to read public sports sites.
- Permission to create a daily routine (asked once).

### What it will not do

- Provide live scores, line-ups, or injury news.
- Access paid or login-only content.
- Guess results or fixtures if sources are unavailable.
- Support multiple teams at once.
