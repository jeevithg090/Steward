---
name: component-planner
description: Plan make-ahead and assemble-at-serve components for a taco bar.
---

1. List all taco components needed for twenty servings. 2. Divide into make-ahead fillings, assemble-at-serve toppings, and shells. 3. Estimate 80 grams of filling per person. 4. For make-ahead fillings, choose three proteins or beans that reheat well. 5. For toppings, list fresh items like lettuce, cheese, and salsa that stay crisp when kept separate. 6. Create a table with columns: component, quantity, make-ahead date, storage method. 7. When an ingredient quantity is unclear, say so and ask the person to confirm. 8. When a make-ahead date is not set, suggest two days before the event.
