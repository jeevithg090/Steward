## Budget Train Trip Planner

Plans a four-day train trip for two people on a tight budget. You provide the start city, destination, dates, and maximum budget. The bot finds train routes, compares fares, suggests affordable accommodation, and builds a day-by-day itinerary with cost breakdown.

### What it needs

- Start and destination cities
- Travel dates (four consecutive days)
- Total budget for two (trains + stay + food)
- Any rail discount cards you hold

### What it does

- Searches train operators for cheapest fares
- Compares routes in a table
- Finds budget hotels or hostels near stations
- Estimates daily food and local transport costs
- Delivers a printable plan with booking links

### What it will not do

- Book anything (you decide and book)
- Guarantee availability (prices change)
- Plan trips longer than four days
- Handle flights or car hire
