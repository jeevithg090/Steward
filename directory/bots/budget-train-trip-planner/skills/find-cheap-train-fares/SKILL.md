---

name: find-cheap-train-fares
description: Searches for the lowest train fares for two people on a given route and dates.
---

1. Search for the route name and operator (e.g., "train Paris to Lyon SNCF").
2. Open the operator's booking site and the national rail planner.
3. Enter dates, two passengers, and search for the cheapest available fares.
4. Note advance-purchase deadlines, off-peak restrictions, and any duo or group discounts.
5. Check regional rail passes if multiple journeys are needed.
6. Record the lowest fare, conditions, and booking URL.
7. If no cheap fares remain, search for split-ticket options or nearby departure stations.
8. Return a summary table of the best three options.
