---

name: budget-accommodation-search
description: Finds affordable double or twin rooms near a station or city centre.
---

1. Search for "cheap hotel near [station name]" and "hostel [city] private room two".
2. Filter for private rooms (not dorms) with prices for two people.
3. Check distance from station and main sights using a map.
4. Verify cancellation policy and whether breakfast or kitchen is included.
5. List 3 to 5 options in a table: name, price per night, distance, booking link.
6. Flag any that require non-refundable prepayment.
7. If nothing under budget, widen search to 2 km from centre or suggest nearby towns with train links.
