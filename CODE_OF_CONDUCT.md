# Code of Conduct

Steward follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text is at that link and applies in full. This page summarises it.

## Our pledge

Everyone who takes part in Steward, as a maintainer, a contributor or someone asking a question,
should be able to do so without harassment, whoever they are and whatever their background, body,
identity, beliefs or experience. We want a community that is open, welcoming, diverse and healthy,
and we will act to keep it that way.

## What we expect

- Kindness, patience and respect, especially when we disagree.
- Feedback that helps, given and taken in good faith.
- Owning our mistakes, apologising to whoever they affected, and learning from them.
- Thinking about what is best for the whole community, not only for ourselves.

## What we do not accept

- Sexual language, imagery, attention or advances.
- Insults, put-downs, trolling, and attacks on someone's person or politics.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Sharing someone's private details, such as where they live or their email address, without their
  clear permission.
- Anything else a reasonable person would see as out of place in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every community space (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat) and when someone
represents the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or see behaviour that breaks this code, report it privately to the maintainers
through the repository's private reporting channel (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for how to reach
us privately), or by contacting a maintainer directly. Every report will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and safety of the person reporting will be
respected.

Maintainers will follow the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines: a correction, a warning,
a temporary ban, or a permanent ban, depending on the impact of what happened.
